The Protection Others Won't Talk About

Insurance Won't Save You. Proof Will.

Most safety software focuses on civil claims. We focus on what protects you when the HSE comes knocking.

Most holiday park operators believe their insurance covers everything. It doesn't. When health and safety failures result in criminal prosecution, you're on your own.

Insurance companies profit from civil liability policies — but they don't cover criminal prosecution. Corporate manslaughter, gross negligence, and HSWA breaches mean personal criminal liability for directors and managers. No insurance policy in the world protects against prison.

Civil Liability

Who pays? Insurance company
At stake? Money (compensation)
Insurable? ✓ Yes

Criminal Liability

Who pays? YOU personally
At stake? Prison, unlimited fines
Insurable? ✗ Never

The majority of management techniques and safety software in the holiday park industry are driven by civil incidents and possible outcomes. This is not the focus. The focus must also be on the decisions and processes that have a criminal outcome — reducing your vicarious liability.

The Cost Calculation Nobody Mentions

It's Not a Cost. It's Tax Deductible.

If you must do it, must need it, and must have it by law — HMRC lets you claim it back.

A plethora of businesses don't include obligatory health and safety as a tax incentive. Consultation fees, software subscriptions, compliance activities, training — it's all deductible.

If legislation requires it, HMRC recognises it as a legitimate business expense. That means your safety software, your compliance consultancy, your training courses, and even the operational activities required to meet regulations can reduce your tax bill.

This changes the entire cost calculation. What looks like an expense on paper becomes part of your cost-neutral compliance strategy over a 3-year KPI cycle.

The HMRC Rule

Expenses incurred "wholly and exclusively" for business purposes — including meeting statutory health and safety obligations — are allowable deductions against taxable profits. Software subscriptions, training costs, safety equipment, professional fees, and compliance activities all qualify.

Your accountant should be including: safety software subscriptions, external consultancy, health and safety training courses, first aid training, fire safety courses, compliance equipment, PPE, signage, testing services, and the staff time spent on mandatory safety activities.

🌿 Example: Grass Cutting = Fire Safety

Cutting grass ensures fire boxes can be seen and accessed. That's not gardening — that's fire safety compliance. Everything involved becomes deductible:

  • Equipment
  • Fuel
  • Man hours
  • Lubricants
  • Strimmer line
  • Maintenance

💧 Example: Hot Tub Water Testing

PWTAG compliance requires regular water testing. The whole regime is tax deductible:

  • Test kits
  • Chemicals
  • UKAS lab fees
  • Staff time
  • Software
  • Training
