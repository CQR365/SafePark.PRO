EQUIPMENT SAFETY RISKS

SWINGS - Medium Risk
  • Entanglement in chains during use - Control: Daily visual inspection of chains and seats
  • Impact injuries from moving swing seats - Control: Maintain clear swing zones, adequate spacing
  • Falls from height during use - Control: Age-appropriate seat heights, proper fall zones
  • Weather-related deterioration of chains/seats - Control: Monthly detailed inspections, immediate repair
SLIDES - High Risk
  • Falls from platform or during descent - Control: Handrails, barriers, proper platform design
  • Burns from hot surfaces in direct sunlight - Control: Temperature monitoring, shade provision
  • Head/limb entrapment in gaps or openings - Control: Gap measurements per BS EN 1176
  • Collision injuries at slide exit - Control: Adequate run-out zones, proper exit design
CLIMBING EQUIPMENT - Medium Risk
  • Falls from climbing structures - Control: Maximum heights per age group, impact-absorbing surfaces
  • Finger/limb trapping in climbing holds - Control: Regular inspection of holds and gaps
  • Equipment failure under load - Control: Load testing, manufacturer specifications compliance
  • Weather-related slip hazards - Control: Non-slip surfaces, drainage considerations
SPRING RIDERS - Low Risk
  • Falls due to excessive movement - Control: Age-appropriate spring tension, fall surfaces
  • Pinch points in spring mechanisms - Control: Spring guards, regular lubrication
  • Multiple user conflicts - Control: Clear capacity signage, supervision requirements

SAFETY INFRASTRUCTURE RISKS

PERIMETER FENCING - Very High Risk if Inadequate
  • Unauthorized access to play area - Control: Minimum 1.2m height fencing, secure gates
  • Children leaving supervised area unseen - Control: Self-closing gates, visual barriers prevention
  • Vandalism and antisocial behavior - Control: CCTV where appropriate, regular patrols
  • Animal access creating health hazards - Control: Small mesh apertures, ground-level barriers
  • Weather damage compromising security - Control: Wind-resistant design, regular structural checks
SAFETY SIGNAGE - High Risk if Missing/Inadequate
  • Age-inappropriate use of equipment - Control: Clear age restriction signage at entry and equipment
  • Lack of emergency contact information - Control: Emergency contact boards, first aid locations
  • Unknown safety standards/compliance - Control: BS EN 1176 compliance certificates displayed
  • Misuse due to unclear rules - Control: Play area rules clearly displayed, multilingual if needed
  • Weather-related sign deterioration - Control: UV-resistant materials, regular replacement schedule
GATE ACCESS CONTROL - Medium Risk
  • Children accessing during closed hours - Control: Self-closing mechanisms, time locks where appropriate
  • Gate hardware failure/wear - Control: Regular lubrication, hinge and latch inspections
  • Emergency access impediment - Control: Emergency release mechanisms, clear access routes
  • Finger trapping in gate mechanisms - Control: Appropriate gap sizing, soft-close mechanisms

ENVIRONMENTAL & OPERATIONAL RISKS

SURFACE CONDITIONS - Medium Risk
  • Inadequate impact absorption - Control: Regular depth checks, maintenance of surface materials
  • Surface contamination (glass, litter, animal waste) - Control: Daily inspection and cleaning
  • Drainage and water accumulation - Control: Proper drainage design, standing water removal
  • Surface displacement creating trip hazards - Control: Regular raking/leveling, edge maintenance
WEATHER CONDITIONS - Low to Medium Risk
  • Ice formation on equipment and surfaces - Control: Winter closure procedures, ice removal protocols
  • High wind affecting equipment stability - Control: Wind speed monitoring, temporary closure procedures
  • UV degradation of equipment and signage - Control: UV-resistant materials, replacement schedules
  • Flooding and waterlogging - Control: Drainage systems, elevated equipment where appropriate
Reference: Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, BS EN 1176:2017, BS EN 1177:2018

Comprehensive Safe Working Procedures

Complete Procedures: These procedures cover all aspects of play park safety management including equipment, infrastructure, signage, and fencing. All procedures comply with BS EN 1176:2017, Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, and Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999.

DAILY INSPECTION PROCEDURES

GENERAL SITE APPROACH
  1. Arrive at site and conduct initial visual sweep from perimeter
  2. Check for obvious hazards, unauthorized persons, or overnight damage
  3. Verify weather conditions are suitable for inspection
  4. Ensure inspection equipment is available (measuring tape, camera, forms)
  5. Review previous inspection reports for any outstanding issues
Reference: Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, Reg. 3
PERIMETER FENCING INSPECTION
  1. Structural Integrity: Walk entire perimeter checking for loose posts, damaged panels, or gaps
  2. Height Compliance: Verify minimum 1.2m height maintained (measure any suspect areas)
  3. Mesh/Panel Security: Check all fixings, clips, and connections are secure
  4. Ground Level: Inspect for undermining, erosion, or gaps under fencing
  5. Vegetation Management: Remove climbing aids (ivy, branches) and check for growth damage
  6. Vandalism/Damage: Document any graffiti, cut mesh, or deliberate damage
  7. Access Points: Verify all unauthorized entry points are properly secured
Reference: BS EN 1176:2017 Section 4.2.10
GATE AND ACCESS CONTROL INSPECTION
  1. Gate Operation: Test all gates open and close smoothly without excessive force
  2. Self-Closing Mechanisms: Verify gates close fully and latch securely from any position
  3. Latch Function: Test latches operate correctly and cannot be opened by young children
  4. Hinge Condition: Check for wear, corrosion, or need for lubrication
  5. Gap Compliance: Measure gaps to ensure no finger trapping risks (max 8mm or min 25mm)
  6. Emergency Access: Confirm emergency services can access if required
  7. Sight Lines: Ensure gates don't obstruct supervision sight lines
Reference: BS EN 1176:2017 Section 4.2.10.2
SIGNAGE INSPECTION AND MAINTENANCE
  1. Safety Signage Verification:
    • Age restriction signs clearly visible and legible
    • Equipment-specific age warnings at each major piece
    • Supervision requirements clearly stated
    • User capacity limitations displayed where relevant
  2. Emergency Information:
    • Emergency contact numbers clearly displayed
    • Site address visible for emergency services
    • First aid procedure information available
    • Nearest hospital/medical facility information
  3. Compliance Information:
    • BS EN 1176:2017 compliance certificate displayed
    • Installation date and installer information
    • Inspection schedule and contact details
  4. Sign Condition Assessment:
    • Check for fading, cracking, or weather damage
    • Verify all text remains legible
    • Confirm mounting security and stability
    • Look for vandalism or unauthorized additions
Reference: BS EN 1176:2017 Section 5.4.3

EQUIPMENT-SPECIFIC INSPECTION PROCEDURES

SWING INSPECTION PROTOCOL
  1. Visually inspect all chain links for wear, corrosion, or damage
  2. Check seat attachment points and seat condition
  3. Verify swing clearance zones are free from obstructions
  4. Test swing movement - should be smooth without binding
  5. Check support structure bolts and connections
  6. Measure chain/rope length compliance with age group
  7. Confirm adequate fall zone surfacing depth and extent
SLIDE INSPECTION PROTOCOL
  1. Check slide surface for cracks, splits, or rough edges
  2. Verify platform barriers and handrails are secure
  3. Test access ladder/steps for stability and secure fixing
  4. Measure platform height compliance with age group
  5. Check slide exit zone is clear and properly surfaced
  6. Verify no head/limb entrapment risks in gaps
  7. Assess slide temperature (may be unsafe in hot weather)
CLIMBING EQUIPMENT PROTOCOL
  1. Inspect all climbing holds and hand/foot grips for security
  2. Check support structure and frame connections
  3. Verify maximum climbing height compliance
  4. Test rope/net climbing elements for wear or damage
  5. Confirm adequate fall zone coverage and depth
  6. Check for pinch points or sharp edges
  7. Verify age-appropriate challenge level and markings

SURFACE AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROCEDURES

IMPACT-ABSORBING SURFACE INSPECTION
  1. Depth Verification: Use probe to check depth at multiple points (min 200mm loose fill)
  2. Contamination Check: Remove glass, litter, animal waste, or foreign objects
  3. Surface Displacement: Rake/level areas where material has been displaced
  4. Edge Containment: Check edging systems are secure and at correct height
  5. Drainage Assessment: Verify no standing water or waterlogged areas
  6. Wear Patterns: Identify high-wear areas requiring additional surfacing
  7. Weed Growth: Remove vegetation growth through surfacing
Reference: BS EN 1177:2018

EMERGENCY AND INCIDENT PROCEDURES

IMMEDIATE HAZARD RESPONSE
  1. Isolate Hazard: Immediately cordon off dangerous equipment or areas
  2. Document Evidence: Photograph hazard and surrounding area
  3. Report Immediately: Contact management and remove from use until repaired
  4. Signage: Place clear warning signs to prevent use
  5. Alternative Arrangements: Consider if alternative play provision needed
  6. Follow-up: Ensure repair completion before returning to service
WEATHER-RELATED CLOSURE PROCEDURES
  1. Ice Conditions: Close facility until ice removal completed safely
  2. High Winds: Close if sustained winds exceed 25mph or gusts exceed 35mph
  3. Flooding: Close until water recedes and safety inspection completed
  4. Lightning Risk: Close during thunderstorm activity within 5 miles
  5. Extreme Heat: Monitor surface temperatures - close metal equipment if >60°C
  6. Post-Weather Inspection: Complete full inspection before reopening
