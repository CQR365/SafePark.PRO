How the Score is Calculated

The risk score is calculated continuously from your assessment answers. Each question is weighted equally. Yes scores 0 risk points (fully compliant), Partial scores 1 point (some deficiency), No scores 3 points (non-compliant — higher weighting reflects increased risk). N/A items are excluded from the calculation. The compliance percentage and risk rating are derived from the total risk points against the maximum possible score.

LOW 85–100% compliance — fire safety measures are generally satisfactory with minor improvements recommended
MEDIUM 65–84% compliance — some deficiencies identified requiring action within a reasonable timescale
HIGH 40–64% compliance — significant deficiencies requiring urgent remedial action
CRITICAL Below 40% compliance — serious and imminent risk to life, immediate action required

⚙ Assessor Override

If professional judgement requires a different risk rating from the calculated score, you may override it here. A justification is required and will be recorded on the assessment.
