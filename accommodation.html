Accommodation Risk Assessment

Comprehensive accommodation safety risk analysis and hazard identification.

Critical Safety Areas: All accommodation units must be assessed for fire safety, gas appliance safety, electrical safety, water safety, structural integrity, carbon monoxide risks, and security vulnerabilities.
Fire Safety Risks
  • Escape route obstructions
  • Faulty smoke alarms
  • Missing fire extinguishers
  • Emergency lighting failures
  • Fire door malfunctions
Gas Safety Risks
  • LPG connection leaks
  • Faulty gas appliances
  • Inadequate ventilation
  • Missing carbon monoxide detectors
  • Gas shut-off valve issues
Electrical Safety Risks
  • RCD protection failures
  • Damaged interior and exterior sockets
  • Exterior lighting system failures
  • Security lighting malfunctions
  • Overloaded circuits
  • Faulty PAT tested appliances
  • Water ingress to exterior electrical systems
  • Exposed exterior electrical connections
Water Safety Risks
  • Legionella contamination
  • Scalding from hot water
  • Water pressure issues
  • Leak damage to structure
  • Poor water quality
Structural Safety Risks
  • Exterior decking and step deterioration
  • Handrail instability and loose fixings
  • Window and door lock failures
  • Door handle and hardware malfunctions
  • Door mat displacement and trip hazards
  • Roof leaks and external damage
  • Foundation settling and exterior wall cracks
  • Access route deterioration
Security Risks
  • Inadequate access control
  • Poor external lighting
  • Vulnerable storage areas
  • Window security issues
  • Guest safety concerns
Risk Assessment Requirements: All identified risks must be documented with mitigation strategies and regular review schedules. High-risk items require immediate action and maintenance requests.

Safe Working Procedures

Standard operating procedures for accommodation maintenance and safety checks.

Pre-Arrival Preparation
  • Complete safety checklist before guest arrival
  • Test all safety systems and equipment
  • Verify cleanliness and presentation standards
  • Check inventory and welcome pack
  • Document any maintenance issues
Gas Appliance Safety Checks
  • Visual inspection of LPG connections
  • Test gas appliance operation
  • Check ventilation grilles are clear
  • Verify carbon monoxide detector function
  • Test gas shut-off valve operation
Electrical System Inspections
  • Test RCD protection operation
  • Check socket and switch condition
  • Verify lighting system function
  • Inspect PAT tested appliances
  • Test emergency lighting systems
Water System Maintenance
  • Check hot water temperature and pressure
  • Inspect for leaks and water damage
  • Test Legionella prevention measures
  • Verify pressure relief valve operation
  • Check water quality and flow rates
Fire Safety Equipment Checks
  • Test smoke alarm batteries and operation
  • Check fire extinguisher pressure
  • Verify escape route accessibility
  • Test emergency lighting systems
  • Inspect fire blanket availability
Emergency Response Procedures
  • Know emergency contact procedures
  • Understand gas emergency shut-off
  • Practice fire evacuation routes
  • Maintain first aid kit supplies
  • Document and report all incidents
Safety Reminder: Never attempt repairs beyond your competency level. Always isolate gas and electrical supplies before maintenance work. Report all safety concerns immediately through the maintenance system.

Daily Accommodation Safety Inspection

Complete the 8-point accommodation safety checklist for your assigned units.

Inspection Location:

8-Point Accommodation Safety Checklist

1. Fire Safety Systems
Smoke alarms functional, fire extinguishers present and pressurized, escape routes clear and accessible, emergency lighting operational, fire blankets available where required.
2. Gas Safety Check
LPG connections secure (interior and exterior), gas appliances operating correctly, ventilation adequate and unobstructed, carbon monoxide detectors functional, gas shut-off valves accessible, exterior gas bottle storage secure and compliant, no gas odors detected.
3. Electrical Safety
RCD protection operational, interior and exterior sockets undamaged, interior and exterior lighting systems functional, exterior security lighting operational, PAT tested appliances safe, no overloading of circuits, exterior electrical connections weatherproof and secure.
4. Water Systems
Hot water temperature appropriate, water pressure adequate, no leaks visible internally or externally, exterior water taps and connections secure, Legionella controls in place, pressure relief valves functional, drainage systems clear and operational.
5. Structural Safety
Doors and windows secure and functional, exterior steps and decking stable and safe, handrails secure and properly fixed, door handles and locks operational, exterior door mats secure and non-slip, no structural damage visible to walls, roof, or foundations.
6. Security Systems
Entry controls functional, window and door locks secure, exterior security lighting operational, access routes well-lit and clear, safe storage areas secure, guest safety measures in place, exterior door handles and locks working properly.
7. Emergency Equipment
First aid kit present and stocked, emergency contact information displayed, communication devices functional, evacuation procedures posted.
8. General Condition
Interior accommodation clean and presentable, exterior areas including decking, steps, and handrails in good condition, door mats clean and properly positioned, access routes clear and safe, exterior lighting functional, no maintenance issues visible inside or outside, guest welfare considerations met, all identified hazards removed or reported.
