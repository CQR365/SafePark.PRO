DirectsSite Supervisors, civils operatives and contractors on civils work. Whether the Project Manager will report to this post is to confirm.
Status of the postIn place and filled. Commercial Manager and Civils Manager are two posts, held by one person at present. The organisation document shows one combined post, Civils and Project Manager; the Project Manager is now a separate post and is not yet in place.

The title Civils Manager carries no definition in law. Marine civils is construction work under CDM 2015 where it is the construction, alteration, repair, upkeep, maintenance or removal of a structure. The regulations list dock, harbour, sea defence works, river works, pipe or pipeline and cable as structures (regulation 2), so harbour and jetty maintenance will usually be construction work. Chartering a vessel is not in itself construction work. On that work the law puts its duties on the Company as a contractor, as a principal contractor where the client appoints it in writing, and as a designer where it prepares or modifies a design, including temporary works. This post is how the Company carries those duties out, so the core functions below are the contractor's duties written as the work of one post.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 for the combined post. The four authority lines have been split between this post and the Project Manager, and the split is proposed. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Stop or suspend project work.
  • Refuse a subcontractor onto a project.
  • No authority to accept a contractual obligation the business cannot deliver safely or within scope.

11. Job advert

Civils Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will lead our marine civils work: harbour, jetty and waterside construction and maintenance delivered from vessels and from the shore. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will make sure every job is planned, resourced, supervised and recorded, and that our people go home safe.

What you will do

  • Decide and record for each job whether it is construction work and what duties we hold
  • Plan, manage and monitor civils work so that it is done safely and to specification
  • Make sure construction phase plans, risk assessments, method statements and permits are in place and briefed
  • Approve, brief and monitor subcontractors
  • Keep site plant and lifting equipment registered, examined and in safe working order
  • Plan lifting operations or make sure a competent person does
  • Put the right supervision, welfare and emergency arrangements on every site, including for work on or near water
  • Lead health and safety in the department and work closely with our vessel masters, divers and compliance team
  • Report incidents and send in routine returns on time

What you will bring

  • Experience of managing marine or waterside civils work
  • A current SMSTS certificate
  • Sound working knowledge of the CDM, lifting and work equipment regulations
  • Experience of planning lifts, or the training to do so
  • Experience of managing people, subcontractors and records to an audited standard

It would help if you also have

  • A qualification in civil engineering or construction management
  • A CSCS manager card and permit issuer training
  • A health and safety management qualification

We support SMSTS refresher training, lift planning and permit training, and membership of a relevant professional body.
