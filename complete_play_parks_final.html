General Site Safety Procedures

Before each inspection period:

  • Ensure all inspection equipment is available and in working order
  • Review previous inspection reports and outstanding maintenance items
  • Check weather conditions are suitable for inspection
  • Confirm inspector competency and training status
  • Notify management of any immediate safety concerns
Reference: Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, Reg. 3
