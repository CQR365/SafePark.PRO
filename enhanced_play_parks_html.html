Automatically Generated Safe Working Procedures

Auto-Generated Procedures: These procedures are automatically created based on your equipment selection and comply with BS EN 1176:2017, Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, and Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999.

General Highland Holidays Site Safety Procedures

Before each inspection period:

  • Ensure all inspection equipment is available and in working order
  • Review previous inspection reports and outstanding maintenance items
  • Check weather conditions are suitable for inspection
  • Confirm inspector competency and training status
  • Notify Highland Holidays management of any immediate safety concerns
Reference: Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, Reg. 3

Equipment-specific procedures will be generated based on your selections in the Setup section.

Automated Inspection Schedules

Compliance Note: Inspection frequencies are automatically set according to BS EN 1176:2017 recommendations and can be adjusted based on risk assessment outcomes and manufacturer requirements.

DAILY VISUAL INSPECTION CHECKLIST

Frequency: Every day the playground is in use | Duration: 15-30 minutes | Competency: Basic trained staff

Check Item Satisfactory Action Required Notes/Actions
General Site Condition
Litter, debris, vandalism, obstructions
Safety Surfacing
Displacement, holes, foreign objects
Equipment Stability
Obvious movement, damage, wear
Moving Parts Operation
Swings, rockers, spinners function correctly
Reference: BS EN 1176-7:2017, Section 4.2.2 - Routine Visual Inspection

WEEKLY FUNCTIONAL INSPECTION

Frequency: Weekly | Duration: 1-2 hours | Competency: Trained maintenance staff

Check Item Good Action Required Details/Actions
Hardware & Fasteners
Tightness, corrosion, wear, protrusions
Surface Depth & Coverage
Measure critical fall heights, rake if required
Lubrication Points
Moving parts, bearings, pivot points
Reference: BS EN 1176-7:2017, Section 4.2.3 - Operational Inspection

MONTHLY DETAILED INSPECTION

Frequency: Monthly | Duration: 2-4 hours | Competency: Qualified inspector

Monthly inspection items will be generated based on equipment selection in Setup.

Reference: BS EN 1176-7:2017, Section 4.2.3 - Operational Inspection (Detailed)

6-MONTHLY TECHNICAL INSPECTION

Frequency: Every 6 months | Duration: 4-8 hours | Competency: Qualified technical inspector

Technical Inspection Requirements: Must be carried out by competent person with appropriate qualifications (RoSPA Inspector, manufacturer trained, or equivalent).
