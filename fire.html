Electrical Equipment Recommendations

Regular PAT testing of all electrical appliances
Install RCD protection on all circuits
Ensure adequate electrical maintenance schedules

Heating Systems Recommendations

Annual gas safety checks for all heating appliances
Install carbon monoxide detectors in heating areas
Maintain clearances around heating equipment

Cooking Equipment Recommendations

Install fire blankets in all cooking areas
Provide adequate ventilation for cooking areas
Regular cleaning and maintenance of cooking equipment

Smoking Materials Recommendations

Designate specific smoking areas away from buildings
Provide proper ashtrays and disposal facilities
Install no-smoking signs in accommodation areas

LPG/Gas Storage Recommendations

Store gas cylinders in well-ventilated areas
Regular inspection of gas connections and pipework
Install gas leak detection systems

Maintenance Work Recommendations

Hot work permit system for welding/cutting operations
Fire watch procedures during maintenance work
Safe storage of flammable materials during work

Escape Routes Recommendations

Ensure all escape routes are clearly marked
Regular inspection of escape route lighting
Keep escape routes clear of obstructions

Fire Safety Equipment Recommendations

Monthly testing of fire alarm systems
Annual servicing of fire extinguishers
Install additional smoke detectors in high-risk areas
