Applicable Legislation & Standards

  • England & Wales: Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022
  • Scotland (SFRS): Fire (Scotland) Act 2005, Fire Safety (Scotland) Regulations 2006
  • Northern Ireland: Fire Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, Building Regulations (NI) 2024
  • Holiday Parks: BS 3632:2015 (Static Caravans), Caravan Sites Act 1968, Model Standards 2008
  • Standards: BS 9991:2024, BS 5839 (Fire Detection), BS 5306 (Fire Extinguishers)

Are appropriate fire extinguishers installed and regularly maintained?

Fire Extinguisher Improvements Required

Install appropriate type and number of fire extinguishers
Schedule annual service for all fire extinguishers
Is electrical installation safe and compliant with current standards?

Electrical Safety Improvements Required

Obtain current electrical installation certificate (EICR)
Replace any faulty or damaged electrical equipment
