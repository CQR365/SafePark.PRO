How to use this site. Open your post. Go through it and mark each line Y or N, with a comment where something should change. Your answers save as you go. When you have finished, open My responses, enter your name and post, and press Send my responses.

Draft tasks: tasks the draft documents give the post. Unowned duties: duties in the post's department documents that name no owner. To confirm: core functions whose source could not be read. Rated HIGH: gaps with a first pass rating of 12 or more.

What could not be verified

  • Workboat Code Edition 3. The manning, safety management, fire and lifesaving parts could not be read. Certificate lines for masters, engineers and crew are marked to confirm and need checking for each vessel.
  • Designated Person Ashore. The ISM Code regulations, as read, make a designated person a legal duty only for certain larger ships. The profiles do not state it as a legal requirement for the Company.
  • The ISO standards and the IMCA documents were not read in full. ISO clause numbers are taken from the organisation document.
  • The HSE approved codes L104 and L103 were read through a web reader. Paragraph numbers should be spot checked before adoption.
  • The new risk assessments were written without sight of the plant, the site or the vessels. They state the controls that should be in place and list what could not be established.
Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd. Prepared by the Risk and Compliance Manager, 6 October 2026. These are current observations only. Where a page says nothing was found, that means no evidence was found, not that the arrangement does not exist.
