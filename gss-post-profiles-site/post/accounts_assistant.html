1. The post

PurposeMaintains the accounting and purchase records that support financial control and the management system. Payroll administration sits with the Office Manager team; this post provides the financial processing behind it.
Reports toFinancial Controller
DepartmentFinance
Stand inTo confirm. None recorded.
DirectsNo one
Status of the postIn place and filled. An existing post, which also does the Silvers accounts. The April 2026 finance sheet lists its tasks under a column headed "New position" and never records the title.

No title in an accounts office has a definition in law, and no qualification is required by law to keep a company's books in house. The duty to keep adequate accounting records is on the Company (Companies Act 2006 section 386), and the duties on VAT, payroll and pay records are on the Company as taxpayer and employer. This post does the processing through which the Company meets them. The money laundering regulations apply to firms that provide accountancy services to other persons, not to a company's own accounts staff. Silvers is a trading name of the Company and not a separate company, so the Silvers accounts are part of the Company's own accounting records and the same duties apply to them.

8. Job advert

Accounts Assistant

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will look after the bank, sales ledger and payments for a marine, diving and boatyard company, including our boatyard and marina accounts. Reporting to the Financial Controller, you will keep cash, customer accounts and cost information accurate and up to date.

What you will do

  • Reconcile bank and company credit card accounts
  • Raise sales invoices and post customer receipts
  • Send customer statements and chase overdue accounts
  • Prepare supplier payment runs for approval
  • Make payroll tax payments to HMRC on time
  • Post expenses and confidential invoices
  • Set up job records for projects in the accounting system
  • Prepare the cash flow report and cost data for managers
  • Keep financial records secure and for the required periods

What you will bring

  • Experience of bank reconciliation, sales ledger and credit control
  • Experience of preparing payment runs under an approval process
  • Confidence with an accounting system and spreadsheets
  • An understanding of payroll accounting and payments to HMRC
  • Accuracy, discretion and a steady approach to deadlines

It would help if you also have

  • A bookkeeping or accounting technician qualification
  • Experience of cash flow or cost reporting
  • Awareness of data protection

We support study towards an accounting technician qualification and the continuing professional development that goes with membership.
