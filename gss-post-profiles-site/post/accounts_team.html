1. The post

PurposeMaintains the accounting and purchase records that support financial control and the management system. Payroll administration sits with the Office Manager team; this post provides the financial processing behind it.
Reports toFinancial Controller
DepartmentFinance
Stand inTo confirm. None recorded.
DirectsNo one
Status of the postIn place and filled. How many people are in the team, and their titles, is to confirm. The drafts call it payroll, the wages department and the Accounts column.

No title in an accounts office has a definition in law, and no qualification is required by law to keep a company's books in house. The duty to keep adequate accounting records is on the Company (Companies Act 2006 section 386), and the duties on VAT, payroll and pay records are on the Company as taxpayer and employer. This post does the processing through which the Company meets them. The money laundering regulations apply to firms that provide accountancy services to other persons, not to a company's own accounts staff.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it. The other Accounts post has the same list.

  • Process transactions within the approved route.
  • No authority to raise a purchase order outside it.

11. Job advert

Accounts Administrator (Accounts Team)

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will join the accounts team of a marine, diving and boatyard company. Reporting to the Financial Controller, you will keep the purchase ledger, payroll processing and VAT records accurate, on time and ready for review.

What you will do

  • Post purchase invoices and match them to approved purchase orders
  • Reconcile supplier statements and the purchase ledger control account
  • Reconcile company credit card statements
  • Process the monthly payroll from timesheets and pay approved expenses
  • Prepare VAT returns from our digital records
  • Keep supplier records in line with our approved supplier list
  • Maintain the purchase order system and project cost spreadsheets
  • Keep financial and payroll records secure and for the required periods

What you will bring

  • Experience of purchase ledger work and reconciliations
  • Confidence with an accounting system and spreadsheets
  • Payroll processing experience
  • A working knowledge of VAT records and returns
  • Accuracy, discretion and the confidence to query what does not match

It would help if you also have

  • A bookkeeping or accounting technician qualification
  • A recognised payroll qualification
  • Awareness of data protection

We support study towards an accounting technician or payroll qualification and the continuing professional development that goes with membership.
