1. The group

Who is in the groupEveryone employed by the Company at any level, ashore and afloat, at GSS and at Silvers. Managers and the Managing Director are in the group as employees. Contractors and self employed people have their own profile, although 41 of the 274 draft tasks are written to both groups.
PurposeAccountable for taking reasonable care of themselves and others at work, cooperating with the Company on health and safety, and following the Company's policies and procedures. (Proposed)
Answers toTheir line manager, or the master when aboard a vessel.
StatusA duty group, not a post. The organisation document has no entry for the group, other than the universal authority to stop work in Part 7.

These duties sit on the individual, not on the Company. Sections 7 and 8 of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and regulation 14 of the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 put duties directly on every employee, and the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 does the same for fire. They do not replace the employer's duties: the Company must still provide safe work, training and equipment. Everything else in the drafts that is addressed to everyone is a Company rule, which employees follow as part of cooperating with their employer.

Authority of the group

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 has no entry for this group. The authority below is the universal authority in Part 7 (general provisions) of that document, which applies to every person. Nothing further is proposed. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this group.

  • Every person working for or on behalf of GSS, without exception and regardless of employment status, has the authority to stop their own work, or work they are involved in, where they believe it is unsafe.
  • No permission is required.
  • Nobody may be disadvantaged for exercising it.
  • The authority does not depend on a post appearing on the organisation chart.
  • A stop work decision is never overturned by the person whose work was stopped. It is reviewed by the accountable post for the area and the outcome recorded.
  • The authority covers a person's own work and work they are involved in. It carries no authority to direct other work, to restart work that has been stopped, or to commit the Company.
