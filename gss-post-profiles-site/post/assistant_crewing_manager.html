1. The post

PurposeSupports the crewing function while working towards full competence in the role. Carries real responsibilities under supervision, with authority limited accordingly, and a documented development plan.
Reports toCrewing Manager
DepartmentCrewing, which includes travel
Stand inNone named. This post is itself the stand in for the Crewing Manager.
DirectsNone
Status of the postIn place and filled. The post is also called Apprentice Crewing Manager.

The remit is the Crewing Manager's. This post stands in for the Crewing Manager and then checks that the department's duties are complete. Neither title is found in any regulation: the legal duties are the Company's, as owner, shipowner and employer, and are delivered through the crewing posts. The core functions below are the Crewing Manager's, shortened to those needed to stand in. The title Apprentice Crewing Manager implies no independent authority, which does not yet match a post that stands in.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Maintain records and flag renewals.
  • Stop their own work, and raise a concern about any crewing record, under the universal authority to stop work.
  • When standing in: the extent of the authority is to be recorded in advance, as is required of any post that deputises. No such record seen.
  • Limit: no independent authority over crewing decisions. An assignment is confirmed by the Crewing Manager. No authority to waive a certification requirement.

11. Job advert

Assistant Crewing Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will support our Crewing Manager in making sure everyone sent to a vessel or a job holds the right certificates and medical. You will keep crew records, track expiry dates and arrange travel, and learn the role so that you can stand in.

What you will do

  • Keep crew records, certificates and assignment records accurate and up to date
  • Track certificate and medical expiry dates and flag renewals in good time
  • Arrange travel and accommodation for crew changes
  • Gather visa and travel cost information for project pricing
  • Help keep the training and competence matrix current
  • Prepare the evidence the Crewing Manager checks before confirming an assignment
  • Stand in for the Crewing Manager as your training allows, within a written limit
  • Follow a written development plan with regular reviews

What you will bring

  • Careful, accurate record keeping
  • Confidence with databases and office software
  • A clear and polite manner with crew, skippers and suppliers
  • Willingness to learn the certificate, medical, employment and travel document rules for seafarers
  • The confidence to raise a concern when a record does not look right

It would help if you also have

  • Experience in crewing, personnel administration or travel booking
  • A business administration or people management qualification
  • Some knowledge of the marine industry

We give you a written development plan, training in right to work checks and maritime manning rules, and a route to the Crewing Manager role.
