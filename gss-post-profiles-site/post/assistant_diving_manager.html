1. The post

PurposeAccountable for standing in for the Diving Manager with the same remit when the Diving Manager is away, and at other times for checking that every duty of the diving department is owned, done and recorded. (Proposed)
Reports toDiving Manager
DepartmentDiving
Stand inNone in the drafts. To confirm who carries the diving contractor duties day to day if the Diving Manager and this post are both away.
DirectsNo one in its own right. When standing in: Diving Supervisors, divers and dive team (employed and self employed), and the Dive Storeman.
Status of the postIn place and filled. The post has no entry in the organisation document and no tasks in the draft procedures.

The title Assistant Diving Manager does not appear in the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 or in the approved code. The law puts its duties on the diving contractor, which is the Company, and the Company carries them out through the Diving Manager. This post is the Company's stand in for that work, so its remit is the Diving Manager's remit. The approved code asks for a clear written reporting and responsibility structure (L104 paragraphs 30 and 76), which is the basis for putting the stand in arrangement in writing. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is never carried by a deputy.

Authority of the post

The list below follows the Diving Manager's authority and applies only while standing in.

  • When standing in, stop or suspend a diving project.
  • When standing in, refuse plant or equipment into a diving project.
  • When standing in, appoint a diving supervisor in writing for a diving operation. To confirm: this needs the stand in arrangement itself to be in writing, signed by the Managing Director.
  • At any time, ask any diving post for the record that shows a duty has been done.
  • No authority reserved to the Managing Director is carried, and there is no authority to permit a dive outside the approved code or the diving project plan.

11. Job advert

Assistant Diving Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will support the Diving Manager in running our diving department and stand in during absence. At other times you will check that every diving duty is owned, done and recorded, so our divers work safely and our records stand up to audit.

What you will do

  • Stand in for the Diving Manager during absence
  • Check that diving project plans, supervisor appointments and diver checks are complete before work starts
  • Check that diver qualifications, medicals and first aid certificates are in date
  • Check that diving plant, breathing gas tests and examination certificates are in date
  • Keep a register of the department's duties and report any gaps
  • Help prepare for client, trade association and certification audits
  • Report incidents and near misses when standing in
  • Work closely with supervisors, divers, vessel masters and the compliance team

What you will bring

  • Experience as a commercial diver with an HSE approved surface supplied qualification
  • Supervisor certification for inland and inshore diving
  • A working knowledge of the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 and the approved codes of practice
  • A current first aid qualification with oxygen administration
  • Experience of keeping records to an audited standard

It would help if you also have

  • Offshore supervisor certification
  • A health and safety management qualification
  • Internal auditor training

We support the continuing professional development that goes with supervisor certification and first aid revalidation, and time acting up alongside the Diving Manager.
