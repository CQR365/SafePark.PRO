1. The post

PurposeSupports the Operations Manager in the day to day running of the fleet and deputises in their absence. Provides the shoreside continuity that keeps vessel certification, maintenance and defect closure moving between visits.
Reports toOperations Manager
DepartmentMarine
Stand inNone named. This post stands in for the Operations Manager and then checks that the department's duties are complete.
DirectsNo one in its own right. When standing in: those the Operations Manager directs, to the extent recorded in writing.
Status of the postIn place and filled. The organisation document calls it Assistant Marine Manager, and one draft form calls it an operations coordinator.

The remit is the Operations Manager's. The title carries no legal definition. The Workboat Regulations 2023 put their duties on the owner and the master, and the health and safety regulations for ships put theirs on the employer; the Company carries them out through the Operations Manager, and through this post when it stands in. The core functions below are carried from the Operations Manager and shortened to those this post supports day to day or must be able to deliver when standing in. Standing in for the Operations Manager does not make the post holder the Designated Person Ashore: that post has its own deputy.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Exercise Operations Manager authority in their absence, to the extent recorded in writing.
  • Take equipment out of service and raise a defect.
  • No authority to defer planned maintenance or remove a vessel from service unless deputising.

11. Job advert

Assistant Operations Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will support the Operations Manager in running our workboat fleet day to day, and stand in when they are away. You will keep certificates, surveys, maintenance and defect closure moving, and be the shoreside point of contact our Masters rely on.

What you will do

  • Track certificate and survey dates across the fleet and book surveys in good time
  • Check that planned maintenance is done and recorded
  • Follow every defect through to repair and return to service
  • Carry out and support onboard inspections and audits
  • Get stores, spares and equipment to the vessels through our purchasing process
  • Pick up open items from Masters' handovers
  • Countersign vessel activity reports
  • Stand in for the Operations Manager when required

What you will bring

  • Experience of workboat operations afloat or in fleet support ashore
  • Knowledge of coding, survey and class requirements, including the Workboat Code Edition 3
  • Marine engineering awareness and experience of planned maintenance and defect management
  • Working knowledge of merchant shipping health and safety, lifting and accident reporting rules

It would help if you also have

  • Inspection or internal auditor training
  • A health and safety qualification
  • A certificate of competency as master or engineer

We support training on the Workboat Code, internal auditing and health and safety, and membership of a relevant professional body.
