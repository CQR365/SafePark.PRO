Stand inTo confirm. No stand in is named in the organisation document or in the drafts.
DirectsSenior Quantity Surveyor (to confirm)
Status of the postIn place and filled. Commercial Manager and Civils Manager are two posts, held by one person at present.

The title Commercial Manager carries no definition in law and no qualification attaches to it. The legal duties that bear on tendering and contracts sit on the Company: to take on construction work only where it has the capability, to prevent bribery, not to collude with competitors, to give its insurers a fair presentation of the risk, and to meet the payment rules for construction contracts. This post is how the Company carries those duties out when it bids for and accepts work.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Submit bids within approved parameters.
  • No authority to claim an accreditation, certification or capability outside its recorded scope, or to accept contract terms not reviewed under the tender process.

11. Job advert

Commercial Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will lead how we win and contract for work: tenders, pre qualification, contract review and client assurance. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will make sure that what we promise in a bid is accurate, insured and deliverable.

What you will do

  • Own the tender process from enquiry to submission
  • Review each enquiry and contract so that we take on only work we can deliver safely
  • Check that every certificate, accreditation and insurance we rely on is current and covers the work
  • Keep our pre qualification scheme entries up to date and matched to what we bid for
  • Maintain a ready pack of standard answers and evidence for client questionnaires
  • Agree with clients how risks are shared on their sites
  • Flow health, safety and environmental requirements down into subcontracts
  • Manage variations, customer feedback and complaints
  • Lead health and safety in the department

What you will bring

  • Experience of bid management and contract administration in marine, civils or construction work
  • A clear understanding of accreditation schemes and what their scopes cover
  • Working knowledge of construction health and safety duties and contract payment rules
  • An understanding of business insurance and its exclusions
  • Accuracy, and the confidence to decline work that does not fit

It would help if you also have

  • A qualification in quantity surveying, commercial management, law or procurement
  • Membership of a relevant professional body
  • Experience of public sector tendering

We support membership of a relevant professional body and the continuing professional development that goes with it.
