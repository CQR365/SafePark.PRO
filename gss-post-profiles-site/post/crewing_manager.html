1. The post

PurposeAccountable for ensuring people assigned to a vessel or task hold the competence, certification and medical fitness their role requires.
Reports toOperations Manager
DepartmentCrewing, which includes travel
Stand inAssistant Crewing Manager, who stands in for the Crewing Manager and then checks that the department's duties are complete. The post is also called Apprentice Crewing Manager and has no authority to confirm an assignment.
DirectsAssistant Crewing Manager
Status of the postIn place and filled

"Crewing Manager" is not a title in any regulation. The law puts the duties on the Company: as owner or operator to man each vessel safely, as shipowner to give every seafarer an employment agreement and enough rest, and as employer not to employ a seafarer without a medical certificate or a person without the right to work. This post is how the Company carries those duties out, so the core functions below are the Company's duties written as the work of one post. Most skippers and divers are self employed, and several of these duties apply to them just the same.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Refuse an assignment where certification, competence or medical fitness cannot be evidenced.
  • No authority to waive a certification requirement.

11. Job advert

Crewing Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will make sure that everyone we send to a vessel or a job holds the competence, certificates and medical fitness the role needs. Reporting to the Operations Manager, you will run crewing, crew records and travel for our workboat fleet, diving and civils teams.

What you will do

  • Crew our vessels and jobs with people who are qualified, fit and rested
  • Verify certificates and medicals before anyone is assigned, and hold the evidence
  • Track expiry dates and arrange renewals and refresher training in good time
  • Keep the training and competence matrix for every post and person
  • Issue seafarer employment agreements and carry out right to work checks
  • Make sure every new start is inducted before work begins
  • Apply the same standard to self employed, agency and contract personnel
  • Arrange travel and accommodation for crew changes
  • Answer client requests for competence evidence

What you will bring

  • Experience of crewing, personnel or training records in a marine or similar regulated industry
  • Working knowledge of seafarer certificates, medicals, employment agreements and hours of rest rules
  • Experience of right to work checks
  • Careful record keeping and confidence with a crew management system
  • An understanding of duties to self employed, agency and contract workers, and familiarity with IMCA competence guidance and client schemes

It would help if you also have

  • A people management qualification
  • Data protection training
  • Seagoing or marine operations experience

We support training in maritime employment and manning rules and membership of a relevant professional body.
