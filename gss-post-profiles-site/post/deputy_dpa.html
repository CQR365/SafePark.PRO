1. The post

PurposeAccountable for standing in for the Designated Person Ashore so that the vessels always have a link to the Managing Director on safety and pollution prevention, and for checking that the duties of that post are complete.
Reports toDesignated Person Ashore. To confirm: this post has no entry.
DepartmentMarine
Stand inNone. This post is the stand in. If both are absent, the Managing Director should name who the vessels contact.
DirectsNo one. When standing in, the post monitors the vessels on the same footing as the Designated Person Ashore.
Status of the postIn place and filled. No written appointment has been seen, and the post has no defined entry and none in the drafts.

The remit is that of the Designated Person Ashore. The title of deputy does not appear in the ISM Code, which speaks of a "designated person or persons", so a company may name more than one. On what was read for this draft, no source makes a designated person or a deputy a legal requirement for a workboat certificated under the Workboat Code Edition 3; Section 31 and Appendix 8 of the Code could not be read, so the legal position is marked To confirm. The core functions below are carried from the Designated Person Ashore and shortened to those a deputy must be able to perform when standing in.

Authority of the post

The extent of deputising authority is recorded in advance and never carries authority reserved to the Managing Director.

  • When standing in, go directly to the Managing Director on any matter of vessel safety or pollution prevention.
  • When standing in, exercise the authority of the Designated Person Ashore to the extent recorded in writing.
  • At any time, raise with the Designated Person Ashore or the Managing Director a duty of the post that is not being done.
  • No authority to act as Designated Person Ashore unless standing in, and no authority reserved to the Managing Director.

11. Job advert

Deputy Designated Person Ashore

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will stand in for our Designated Person Ashore, so that our vessels always have a direct link to the Managing Director on safety and pollution prevention. You will also take part in vessel inspections and check that the duties of the role are complete.

What you will do

  • Stand in for the Designated Person Ashore when absent
  • Receive incident, defect and safety reports from the vessels and see them acted on
  • Take safety concerns straight to the Managing Director when standing in
  • Act as a shore contact when a vessel has an emergency
  • Take part in onboard inspections and audits
  • Check that the duties of the role are complete and report what is not
  • Record a handover at the start and end of each period of cover

What you will bring

  • A ship's officer certificate, or a relevant higher qualification, or senior ship management experience
  • Designated person training, including audit technique
  • Working knowledge of the Workboat Code Edition 3 and of workboat operations
  • Knowledge of marine accident reporting

It would help if you also have

  • Experience of a marine management system audit
  • Internal auditor training

We support designated person training, audit experience and membership of a relevant professional body.
