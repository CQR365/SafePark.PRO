1. The post

PurposeAccountable for giving the vessels a direct link to the Managing Director on safety and pollution prevention, and for monitoring the safety and pollution prevention aspects of the operation of each vessel.
Reports toManaging Director. Direct access to the Managing Director is the point of the post.
DepartmentMarine
Stand inDeputy Designated Person Ashore, who stands in for the Designated Person Ashore and then checks that the duties of the post are complete.
DirectsDeputy Designated Person Ashore. The post monitors the vessels and those who manage them. It does not direct their day to day work.
Status of the postIn place and filled. A written appointment has not been seen: the organisation document lists it as "DPA appointment, to be confirmed".

The title comes from clause 4 of the International Safety Management (ISM) Code. That clause is law in the United Kingdom for the ships listed in regulation 5(2) of the ISM Code Regulations 2014 (certain passenger ships, and cargo ships and mobile offshore drilling units of 500 gross tonnage or more on international voyages). A workboat certificated under the Workboat Code Edition 3 is not in that list. The Workboat Code Edition 3 does require a safety management system, by 13 December 2026 according to MGN 710 (M), but Section 31 and Appendix 8 of the Code could not be read for this draft and MGN 710 (M) does not mention a designated person. So, on what was read, the Company holds this post by its own choice or because clients ask for it, and the legal position is marked To confirm. The duty to run the vessels safely stays with the Company and the Master; this post monitors and reports.

Authority of the post

One authority line is placed here and the rest with the Operations Manager. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Proposed: go directly to the Managing Director on any matter of vessel safety or pollution prevention, without going through the line.
  • Remove a vessel from service on safety or certification grounds.
  • Proposed limit: the post monitors and reports. It does not direct the day to day operation of a vessel, and it does not displace the authority of the Master on board.

11. Job advert

Designated Person Ashore

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will be the link between our vessels and the Managing Director on safety and pollution prevention. You will monitor how each vessel is operated, make sure the crews get the shore support they need, and see that incidents and defects are acted on.

What you will do

  • Monitor the safety and pollution prevention aspects of each vessel's operation
  • Take safety concerns straight to the Managing Director
  • Receive incident, defect and safety meeting reports from the vessels and see them closed
  • Run a planned programme of onboard inspections and audits
  • See that marine accidents are reported to the authorities on time
  • Support Masters in using their authority on board
  • Act as a shore contact when a vessel has an emergency
  • Help bring our safety management system into line with the Workboat Code Edition 3
  • Share lessons from incidents across the fleet

What you will bring

  • A ship's officer certificate, or a relevant higher qualification, or senior ship management experience
  • Designated person training, including audit technique
  • Experience of workboat operations and of at least one marine management system audit
  • Working knowledge of the Workboat Code Edition 3
  • Knowledge of marine accident reporting and investigation

It would help if you also have

  • Internal auditor training
  • A health and safety management qualification
  • Membership of a relevant professional body

We support refresher training for the role, audit experience and membership of a relevant professional body.
