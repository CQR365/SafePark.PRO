1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the dive store and the diving plant in it: that every item is registered, maintained, examined and tested when due, fit for use when issued, and backed by a certificate the supervisor can see before the operation begins. (Proposed)
Reports toDiving Manager
DepartmentDiving
Stand inNone in the drafts. To confirm who issues and checks diving plant when the post holder is away.
DirectsNo one
Status of the postIn place and filled. The post has no entry in the organisation document and no tasks in the draft procedures.

Dive Storeman is not a title in any regulation or approved code. The law puts the duty for diving plant on the Company as diving contractor: plant must be suitable, sufficient, available when needed and maintained in a safe working condition (Diving at Work Regulations 1997 regulation 6(3)(b) and (c)), and the approved code expects a planned maintenance system (L104 paragraph 122). Other duties sit on the Company as employer, user or owner under the regulations on work equipment, pressure systems, lifting equipment and hazardous substances. This post is how that work gets done day to day. Examinations under a written scheme and thorough examinations of lifting gear are done by outside competent persons; the storeman's part is to keep the register, present the plant, hold the reports and keep out of use anything that is not in date.

Authority of the post

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 has no entry for this post, so the list below is proposed.

  • Withdraw from use, tag and quarantine any item of diving plant that is defective or out of date for examination or test.
  • Refuse to issue plant to a project without a current certificate.
  • Stop the filling of breathing air if the last air quality test is out of date or has failed.
  • Arrange routine servicing and consumables within a limit to be set by the Diving Manager. Limit to confirm.
  • No authority to modify compressors, filtration or life support equipment, to extend an examination date, or to release plant that has failed a test. Those decisions go to the Diving Manager and the competent person.

11. Job advert

Dive Storeman

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run our dive store and look after the diving plant our teams rely on. Reporting to the Diving Manager, you will keep every item maintained, examined, tested and certificated, and make sure nothing goes to a job unless it is fit for use.

What you will do

  • Keep the diving plant register and planned maintenance records up to date
  • Maintain compressors, breathing apparatus, umbilicals and diving equipment
  • Arrange breathing air quality tests and keep the results
  • Present cylinders, pressure systems and lifting gear for examination and test when due
  • Send gauges and gas analysers for calibration
  • Issue plant with current certificates for each job and check it back in
  • Quarantine anything defective or out of date
  • Handle and store compressed gases, oxygen and oils safely
  • Keep dive site first aid kits and emergency oxygen stocked

What you will bring

  • Experience of maintaining commercial diving plant and running a store to a planned maintenance system
  • Training on breathing air compressors and filtration
  • A working knowledge of breathing air quality testing
  • Training in the safe handling and filling of gas cylinders, including oxygen
  • A working knowledge of examination intervals for pressure systems and lifting equipment

It would help if you also have

  • Maker's technician training on diving helmets, masks and regulators
  • A commercial diving background
  • First aid at work

We support maker's training on the plant we use and refresher training in manual handling and hazardous substances.
