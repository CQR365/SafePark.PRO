1. The post

PurposeAccountable for diving, tending and standing by safely and competently under the direction of the appointed supervisor, for holding the qualification and medical certificate the law requires, and for keeping a personal record of every dive. (Proposed)
Reports toThe Diving Supervisor appointed for the operation, and through the supervisor to the Diving Manager.
DepartmentDiving
Stand inNot a single post. The team for each project is set by the diving project plan, and a standby diver is part of every team.
DirectsNo one. A diver or team member may be named for a task in an emergency, such as making the emergency calls.
Status of the postA team, not one post: working divers, standby divers, tenders, and team members with first aid or diver medic duties. Most are self employed and engaged for each project. Numbers employed and self employed: to confirm. The team has no entry in the organisation document.

Regulations 12 and 13 of the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 put duties directly on each diver, and on every person engaged in a diving project, as individuals. They apply whether the person is employed or self employed: HSE states that the regulations cover all dives when one or more divers are at work, employed or self employed. So a self employed diver engaged by the Company answers in person for holding a valid approved qualification and medical certificate, for being competent and fit on the day, for keeping a daily record, and for following the supervisor's directions. The Company, as diving contractor, must still check all of this before the diver enters the water, and must make sure people it does not employ are competent and know its procedures and plant (L104 paragraphs 87 and 88). That is why the engagement checks before first work, the insurance evidence and the Company induction matter for this team more than for any other.

Authority of the post

The list below follows the regulations and the approved code.

  • Decline to dive when unfit, or when not competent for the task. The regulations forbid the dive in both cases.
  • Tell the supervisor of any doubt about fitness, plant or conditions before the dive.
  • Stop work and ask for the dive to be ended on safety grounds. The supervisor decides.
  • Withdraw plant found defective at the check before the dive and report it to the supervisor.
  • No authority to start a dive, to change the diving project plan, or to act against a direction of the supervisor.

11. Job advert

Commercial Diver

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will work as part of our inshore dive team as working diver, standby diver or tender, under the direction of the appointed supervisor. Work includes inspection, construction support and harbour and jetty maintenance. We engage both employed and self employed divers.

What you will do

  • Dive, stand by and tend on surface supplied and SCUBA operations
  • Work to the diving project plan and the supervisor's directions
  • Check your diving plant before every dive and report defects
  • Tell the supervisor of anything that affects your fitness to dive
  • Keep your personal diver logbook up to date
  • Carry out underwater inspections and record what you find
  • Give first aid and oxygen, and take a named part in the emergency plan
  • Report occurrences and near misses at once

What you will bring

  • An HSE approved diving qualification valid for the work
  • A valid certificate of medical fitness to dive
  • A current first aid qualification with oxygen administration
  • An up to date personal diver logbook showing recent commercial dives
  • If self employed, evidence of insurance, and readiness to complete our engagement checks and induction before first work

It would help if you also have

  • A diver medic qualification
  • An approved offshore diving qualification
  • Experience in underwater inspection, lifting or construction tasks

We assess divers regularly, support first aid revalidation, and offer a route towards supervisor certification for those with the experience.
