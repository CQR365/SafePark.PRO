1. The post

PurposeAccountable for diving operations, diving plant and the diving safety management system, and for compliance with the Diving at Work Regulations and the approved code of practice. (GSS-COM-ORG-001)
Reports toManaging Director
DepartmentDiving
Stand inAssistant Diving Manager, who stands in for the Diving Manager and then checks that the department's duties are complete.
DirectsDiving Supervisors, divers and dive team (employed and self employed), Dive Storeman
Status of the postIn place and filled

The title Diving Manager does not appear in the Diving at Work Regulations 1997. The law puts its duties on the diving contractor, which is the Company. This post is how the Company carries those duties out, so the core functions below are the diving contractor's duties written as the work of one post.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Stop or suspend a diving project.
  • Appoint and remove diving supervisors in writing.
  • Refuse plant or equipment into a diving project.
  • No authority to permit a dive outside the approved code or the project plan.

11. Job advert

Diving Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run our diving department and carry the Company's duties as a diving contractor day to day. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will make sure every diving project is properly planned, resourced, supervised, equipped and recorded, and that our divers go home safe.

What you will do

  • Plan diving projects and make sure a risk based diving project plan is in place before work starts
  • Appoint competent supervisors in writing and put the right team on every job
  • Check that every diver holds the right qualification and a valid medical before diving
  • Keep diving plant, breathing gas systems and test certificates in date under a planned maintenance system
  • Make sure emergency and recompression arrangements are in place for every project
  • Keep the diving records the law requires, and report and investigate incidents
  • Lead health and safety in the department and work closely with our vessel masters, clients and compliance team
  • Manage our trade association membership and audits
  • Engage, check and develop employed and self employed divers

What you will bring

  • Experience as a commercial diver with an HSE approved surface supplied qualification
  • Supervisor certification for inland and inshore diving, with experience supervising surface supplied operations
  • A sound working knowledge of the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 and the approved codes of practice
  • A current first aid qualification with oxygen administration
  • Experience of managing people, plant and records to an audited standard

It would help if you also have

  • Offshore supervisor certification
  • A health and safety management qualification
  • Knowledge of pressure systems, lifting equipment and breathing air standards

We support the continuing professional development that goes with supervisor certification, first aid revalidation and membership of a relevant professional body.
