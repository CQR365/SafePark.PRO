1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the safe conduct of the diving operation for which the post holder is appointed in writing, in line with the law and the diving project plan, and for the record of that operation.
Reports toDiving Manager. The appointment for each diving operation is made in writing by the Company as diving contractor.
DepartmentDiving
Stand inAnother supervisor appointed in writing. Only one supervisor may be in charge of an operation at a time, and a handover is entered in the diving operation record.
DirectsEveryone taking part in the diving operation, and anyone who may affect its safety, for as long as the operation lasts.
Status of the postTo confirm. The role is filled for each diving operation by written appointment. Whether the Company holds it as a standing post, and whether its supervisors are employed or self employed, is not shown in the data. The post has no defined entry.

Diving Supervisor is a title with a legal meaning. Regulations 9, 10 and 11 of the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 put duties and a power directly on the supervisor as an individual, not on the Company. The Company, as diving contractor, must appoint one supervisor in writing for each diving operation (regulation 6), and no one may be appointed or act as supervisor unless competent and, where appropriate, suitably qualified (regulation 9). Once appointed, the supervisor answers in law for the conduct of that operation, and may give reasonable directions that override the normal line of management (L104 paragraph 138).

Authority of the post

The list below is taken from the regulations and the approved code, which give the supervisor authority directly.

  • Order the start of a dive. No one else may do so.
  • Stop or end a dive at any time.
  • Give reasonable directions to any person taking part in the diving operation, or who may affect its safety, including people senior to the supervisor in the Company or the client.
  • Refuse to start if the site, the weather, the team, the plant or the emergency arrangements are not as the plan requires.
  • Authorise any vessel entering the dive zone, as the draft method statement puts it.
  • No authority to dive while supervising, to act without a written appointment, or to depart from the diving project plan except where following it would put safety or the law at risk.

11. Job advert

Diving Supervisor

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will supervise our inshore diving operations under a written appointment for each job. You will be in sole charge of the dive: briefing the team, checking people and plant, running the operation to plan, keeping the record and leading any emergency response.

What you will do

  • Supervise surface supplied and SCUBA diving operations to the diving project plan
  • Brief the team on the plan before every operation
  • Check diver qualifications, medicals and fitness before anyone enters the water
  • See that diving plant is inspected and in date before use
  • Complete and sign the diving operation record for every diving day
  • Work closely with the vessel master when diving from a vessel
  • Take charge of the diving response in an emergency
  • Report occurrences and near misses the same day
  • Assess divers and help them develop

What you will bring

  • An HSE approved diving qualification for the techniques you will supervise
  • Supervisor certification for inland and inshore diving
  • A current first aid qualification with oxygen administration
  • A sound working knowledge of the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 and the inshore approved code
  • Experience of supervising surface supplied diving teams

It would help if you also have

  • Competence in operating a recompression chamber
  • Offshore air diving supervisor certification
  • A supervisory health and safety qualification

We support the yearly professional development that keeps supervisor certification valid, and first aid revalidation.
