Status of the postIn place and filled. The post holder also holds the office of Company Secretary and is not a director.

The title Financial Controller has no definition in law, and the law does not require the holder to be a qualified accountant. The Companies Act 2006 puts the duty to keep adequate accounting records on the Company (section 386) and the duty to prepare, approve and file the accounts on its director (sections 394, 414 and 441). The tax, payroll and insurance duties sit on the Company as taxpayer and employer. GOV.UK guidance is plain that a director who hires someone to do this work stays legally responsible for the records, the accounts and the Company's performance. This post is how the Company carries those duties out, so the core functions below are the Company's and the Managing Director's financial duties written as the work of one post.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Approve purchase orders and payments within a recorded limit.
  • Refuse payment to a supplier not on the approved supplier index.
  • Bind the Company on insurance and financial matters as delegated by the Managing Director.
  • No authority above the recorded limit or outside what the Managing Director has delegated. The limit and the delegation are to confirm: no schedule of authorities was seen, and the drafts show the limits as proposed.

11. Job advert

Financial Controller

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

Reporting to the Managing Director, you will lead our finance function. You will keep our accounts, tax, payroll and insurance right, control who may commit our money, and give the business the figures it needs. The post also holds the office of Company Secretary.

What you will do

  • Keep the accounting records and prepare the annual accounts for approval
  • Run budgets, management accounts, cash flow and job costing
  • Oversee payroll, VAT, Corporation Tax and construction industry returns
  • Administer the insurance programme and its declarations
  • Control purchasing authority, payment approval and supplier financial checks
  • Budget for training and for agreed safety and quality actions
  • Support tender pricing and contract terms
  • Keep the Company's statutory filings and records as Company Secretary
  • Lead and develop the accounts and purchasing staff

What you will bring

  • A recognised accountancy qualification
  • Experience of financial control, budgeting and management accounting
  • Working knowledge of PAYE, VAT, Corporation Tax and the Construction Industry Scheme
  • An understanding of insurance, disclosure and policy terms
  • Experience of authority limits, supplier checks and credit assessment

It would help if you also have

  • Job costing experience in contracting or marine work
  • Records management and data protection awareness
  • A health and safety management course for managers

We support the continuing professional development your accountancy body requires, and training in company secretarial practice.
