1. The group

Who is in the groupThe people appointed to carry out internal audits of the management system. In 2026 four audits were delivered by an external provider and four in house. A cross department model is proposed in which managers audit departments other than their own.
PurposeAccountable for auditing the area assigned to them objectively and impartially, and for reporting what the evidence shows. (Proposed)
Answers toThe Risk and Compliance Manager, who designs and runs the audit programme. For an audit of that post's own areas the auditor should answer to the Managing Director (proposed).
StatusA duty group, not a post. The organisation document has no entry for the group. The cross department model is proposed and not yet adopted.

Internal auditor is not a title defined in law. The duty to run internal audits comes from clause 9.2 of each of the three ISO standards the Company is certificated to, and it sits on the organisation. ISO 19011 gives guidance on how audits are run and on the competence of auditors; it is guidance and not a certification requirement. An auditor is whoever the programme appoints for a given audit, and the one fixed rule is independence: auditors do not audit their own work.

Authority of the group

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 has no entry for this group, so the list is proposed. It draws on the authority that document gives the Risk and Compliance Manager, under whose programme auditors act. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by an auditor.

  • See any site, vessel, record or system inside the scope of the audit they are assigned (proposed, under the programme).
  • Speak to any person doing the work audited, and observe the work (proposed).
  • Record a finding against any department and state the evidence for it (proposed).
  • Decline an audit assignment where they are not independent of the work (proposed).
  • Stop their own work, or work they are involved in, where they believe it is unsafe. No permission is required and nobody may be disadvantaged for exercising it (the universal authority in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Part 7).
  • No authority to direct operational work, to audit their own work, to close a finding on their own area, or to change a controlled document.

7. The ISO management system: what it needs from this group

The Company is certificated to three ISO standards and internal audit is a requirement of each; the programme covers the certificated scope and also Silvers, which is inside the management system but outside the certificated scope. Each standard is described in one sentence, followed by what it needs from this group.

StandardWhat it isWhat it needs from this group
ISO 9001, quality managementThe standard for delivering what the client was promised, the same way every time, and improving how that is done.To confirm. The organisation document has no entry for auditors. Proposed: audits carried out as scheduled, objectively and impartially, with results reported to the managers concerned and records kept (9.2).
ISO 45001, occupational health and safetyThe standard for preventing injury and ill health at work, with workers involved in how that is done.To confirm. Proposed: the same as for ISO 9001 (9.2), with relevant results made known to workers and findings passed into incident and corrective action (10.2).
ISO 14001, environmental managementThe standard for knowing how the Company's work affects the environment, controlling it and meeting environmental law.To confirm. Proposed: environmental audits carried out as scheduled, never by a person auditing their own work, with the report completed and signed (9.2).
