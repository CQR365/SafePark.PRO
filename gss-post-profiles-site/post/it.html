1. The post

PurposeAccountable for keeping the Company's information systems, accounts and data available and secure, for the technical controls behind the Cyber Essentials certificate, and for the technical response to a cyber incident or an outage.
Reports toTo confirm. There is no entry for this function. The drafts pair this function with the Risk and Compliance Manager for incident reports.
DepartmentSecurity and IT
Stand inTo confirm. None recorded.
DirectsNo one. The drafts have this function coordinate the other IT suppliers during an outage.
Status of the postTo confirm whether in house or contracted. The drafts refer both to a Company IT function and to an IT consultant working under a maintenance agreement.

The law defines no IT post and requires no qualification for one. The legal duty to keep personal data secure sits on the Company as controller (UK GDPR Articles 5(1)(f) and 32), and this function is how the Company meets the technical part of it. If the work is contracted out, the provider is likely to be the Company's processor and must work under a written contract (Article 28), and the Company stays accountable. Cyber Essentials is a certification scheme backed by government, not a legal requirement; under its rules the certified organisation is always responsible for the controls, even where a supplier puts them in place.

Authority of the post

The list below is for the Managing Director to confirm.

  • Create, change and remove accounts and access on an authorised request.
  • Isolate or disable an account or device that is suspected of being compromised, and report it at once.
  • Refuse to install software or connect equipment that has not been approved.
  • No authority to grant access to a system or to data without the approval of the post that owns it, to decide whether a personal data breach is notified, or to commit spend beyond a limit the Managing Director sets.

11. Job advert

IT Administrator

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will keep the IT of a marine, diving and boatyard company secure and working, across our offices and vessels. You will manage accounts, devices, email, backups and security, keep our cyber certificate in place and lead the technical response when something fails.

What you will do

  • Create, change and remove user accounts, email and distribution groups
  • Keep devices, software and firewalls configured, supported and updated
  • Run multi factor sign in and malware protection across all devices
  • Hold backups apart from the network and test them by restoring
  • Buy and install approved hardware and software
  • Remove access and Company email when people or devices leave
  • Manage vessel account access at each handover
  • Lead the technical response to cyber incidents and outages
  • Supply the evidence for our yearly cyber certificate renewal

What you will bring

  • Experience of administering IT for a small or medium organisation
  • A working knowledge of the Cyber Essentials requirements
  • An understanding of data protection security and breach reporting duties
  • Experience of incident response and restoring from backup
  • Clear records and a calm, methodical approach under pressure

It would help if you also have

  • A recognised technical certification
  • Awareness of vessel communications and control systems
  • Experience of working with outside IT suppliers

We support technical certification and the training needed to keep pace with changes to the cyber security requirements.
