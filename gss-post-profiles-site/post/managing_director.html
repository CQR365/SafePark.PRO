DirectsThe department managers, the Risk and Compliance Manager and the Company Secretary. Engages the shareholder consultants and the retained external advisers.
Status of the postIn place and filled. Sole director of the Company.

The title Managing Director is not defined in the Companies Act 2006. The law speaks of a director, and a private company must have at least one who is a natural person (sections 154 and 155). The general duties in sections 171 to 177 are owed by the Managing Director personally. The health and safety duties in the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 sit on the Company as employer, and the Managing Director answers personally where an offence by the Company is committed with the Managing Director's consent or connivance or through the Managing Director's neglect (section 37). As sole director this post is the whole of the Company's top management, so the core functions below are the Managing Director's own duties and the Company's duties written as the work of one post.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide, and the limit on it. The first five items are reserved to the Managing Director and are not carried by any other post or by anyone standing in.

  • Approve the management system policy, the organisation chart and the certified scope.
  • Appoint competent persons under regulation 7, and withdraw an appointment.
  • Approve the annual objectives and the resources to deliver them.
  • Commit the Company financially without internal limit, and sign contracts on its behalf. In practice this means signing contracts above the limits delegated to other posts.
  • Accept residual risk on behalf of the Company where a risk cannot be eliminated.
  • Suspend any operation, site or vessel.
  • Limit: the Company's articles of association and the Managing Director's duties in sections 171 to 177 of the Companies Act 2006. The articles were not seen, so any matter they reserve to the shareholders is to confirm.

StandardWhat it isWhat it needs from this post

ISO 14001, environmental managementThe standard for knowing how the Company's work affects the environment, controlling it and meeting environmental law.Leadership and commitment (5.1), an approved scope (4.3), environmental objectives set (6.2) and the management review chaired (9.3).

8. Job advert

Managing Director

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will lead the Company as its director. You will hold the employer's duty for the safety of our people, set direction and objectives, provide the resources to meet them, and answer to the shareholders for a safe, lawful and well run marine business.

What you will do

  • Set strategy and annual objectives, and review performance against them
  • Lead health and safety from the top and review it formally every year
  • Appoint competent people in writing and give them the time and resources they need
  • Approve the management system policy, the organisation and the certificated scope
  • Chair the management review and see findings through to a close
  • Decide which work to tender for and sign major contracts
  • Approve the accounts and keep the Company's legal filings in order
  • Keep insurance in line with what the business does
  • Lead the response to a serious incident or business interruption

What you will bring

  • Eligibility to act as a company director, with identity verified at Companies House
  • Director level health and safety training, for example IOSH Leading Safely or equivalent
  • A sound knowledge of directors' duties and of an employer's health and safety duties
  • Working knowledge of the leadership requirements of ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001
  • Commercial, contract and insurance awareness, with senior experience in marine, diving or civils work

It would help if you also have

  • A director qualification, such as a Certificate or Diploma in Company Direction
  • Auditing awareness

We support director development, including health and safety leadership training and membership of a professional body for directors.
