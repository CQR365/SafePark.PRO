1. The post

PurposeAccountable on board for the safety of the vessel, her crew and any persons carried, and for compliance with the safety management system afloat.
Reports toOperations Manager
DepartmentMarine operations (vessels)
Stand inNone named. On a change of master the relieving master takes command after a recorded handover. Who takes charge if the master is incapacitated at sea is to confirm for each vessel.
DirectsThe vessel engineer, the vessel crew and, for safety, every other person on board (divers, client staff, contractors and visitors).
Status of the postIn place. One master is in command of each vessel when it works. Most skippers are self employed. The number of masters, and which are employed, is to confirm.

The Merchant Shipping Act 1995 (section 313) defines the master as every person, except a pilot, having command or charge of a ship. "Skipper" has no separate meaning in law for a workboat: it is the same post. The health and safety regulations for ships put duties on the employer, on the Company as owner or operator, and on the master. Being self employed does not change the master's duties on board, and it does not remove the Company's duties as owner. What certificate the master must hold is set by the Workboat Code and depends on the vessel and how far from a safe haven it works.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Overriding authority for the safety of the vessel, her crew and any persons carried, and for pollution prevention.
  • Refuse to sail, or to continue, on safety grounds.
  • Take any equipment out of service.
  • Incur expenditure in an emergency, reported at the first opportunity.
  • Refuse to accept a person on board who is not certificated or fit for the role.
  • Limit: no authority to work the vessel outside her certificate, her area category or her required manning, or to waive a certificate or medical requirement.

11. Job advert

Workboat Master

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will take command of one of our workboats, working up to 150 miles offshore in support of marine construction, diving, towing and survey. Reporting to the Operations Manager, you are responsible on board for the vessel, her crew and everyone carried.

What you will do

  • Take command of the vessel and decide on safety, with the authority to refuse to sail
  • Plan each passage and job: weather, tides, stability, manning and hours of rest
  • Induct and brief everyone who comes on board
  • Lead towing, anchor handling, lifting, personnel transfer and support to diving
  • Check that crew certificates and medicals are in date before work starts
  • Keep the log books, hours of rest and vessel records up to date
  • Hold drills and safety meetings and record them
  • Report accidents, near misses and defects on the day
  • Hand over properly at the end of every trip

What you will bring

  • A certificate of competency valid for commercial use on the vessel and area worked (we will confirm the exact certificate for each vessel)
  • A valid seafarer medical: ENG 1 for work beyond 60 miles, or ML5 for work closer in
  • Sea survival, fire fighting, first aid and radio operator certificates
  • Command experience on workboats, with towing, anchor handling or lifting
  • Stability and radar training where the vessel needs them

It would help if you also have

  • Master Workboat less than 500GT certificate
  • A towing endorsement
  • A health and safety qualification for supervisors

We support certificate revalidation, refresher training and progression to higher workboat certificates.
