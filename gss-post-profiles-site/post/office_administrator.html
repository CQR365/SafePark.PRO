1. The post

PurposeSupports the office function, maintaining records, returns and correspondence so that the central registers stay current between reviews. (GSS-COM-ORG-001)
Reports toOffice Manager
DepartmentAdministration
Stand inNone. The post supports the Office Manager. It does not stand in for the Office Manager or for any other post.
DirectsNo one
Status of the postIn place and filled. The drafts and the organisation document call it Office Assistant (code OFA).

The title Office Administrator has no definition in law and no qualification attaches to it. The legal duties on records and personal data sit on the Company as employer and as controller of personal data. This post handles those records day to day on the Office Manager's instructions, and carries the duties every employee has for their own and others' health and safety.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Distribute controlled documents to the current distribution list, and withdraw superseded copies.
  • No approval authority.

11. Job advert

Office Administrator

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will support our Office Manager in the office of a marine, diving and boatyard company. You will keep records, returns and correspondence in order so that our registers stay current and the right document reaches the right person.

What you will do

  • File and keep records to our retention schedule
  • Issue, collect and log routine returns from each department
  • Book in visitors and keep site access records
  • Update certificate and training records as evidence arrives
  • Handle correspondence and the switchboard
  • Send out current documents and withdraw old copies
  • Handle personal information carefully and report any loss at once
  • Help our emergency response team with calls, logs and information when needed

What you will bring

  • Experience of office administration, filing and correspondence
  • Awareness of data protection and good record keeping
  • Confidence with office systems and close attention to detail
  • A clear telephone manner and a careful approach to who you pass information to

It would help if you also have

  • A business administration qualification
  • First aid at work or fire warden training
  • Experience of controlled documents in an audited management system

We give training in data protection, document control and emergency response, and support a business administration qualification.
