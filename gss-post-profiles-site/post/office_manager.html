Reports toManaging Director

Stand inNone recorded. The Office Administrator supports this post but does not stand in for it. To confirm.
DirectsOffice Administrator
Status of the postIn place and filled. The drafts also call this post Personnel Manager, HR, Personnel, Personnel lead, the office, and the holder of personnel records.

The title Office Manager has no definition in law. The duties come from two places. Employment, pay, working time and immigration law put duties on the employer, which is the Company. The UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 put duties on the controller of personal data, which is also the Company. This post is how the Company carries out the people and records side of those duties day to day, so the core functions below are the employer's and the controller's duties written as the work of one post.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Maintain and control the central registers.
  • Withdraw a superseded document from circulation.
  • No authority to approve or issue a controlled document.

11. Job advert

Office Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run our office and hold the personnel function for a marine, diving and boatyard company. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will keep our people records, payroll administration and central registers accurate, secure and ready for audit.

What you will do

  • Run the office and direct the office administration team
  • Keep personnel files, induction records and absence records accurate and secure
  • Carry out right to work checks and issue written statements of particulars to new starters
  • Administer payroll and the hours and absence records behind it
  • Keep the vehicle register and driver authorisations up to date
  • Keep supplier and insurance files current, including contractor insurance
  • Issue, collect and chase the monthly returns from each department
  • Keep records to the retention schedule and handle personal data lawfully
  • Lead health and safety in the office and support our emergency response team

What you will bring

  • Experience of running an office with personnel records, payroll administration and registers
  • A working knowledge of data protection law as it applies to employment records
  • Payroll competence, with awareness of working time and rest hour records
  • A working knowledge of right to work checks and written employment particulars
  • Accuracy, discretion and the confidence to chase managers for what is due

It would help if you also have

  • A CIPD qualification in people practice, or CIPD membership
  • A recognised payroll qualification
  • A health and safety management course for managers

We support professional membership and the continuing professional development that goes with it, and training in data protection, payroll and health and safety.
