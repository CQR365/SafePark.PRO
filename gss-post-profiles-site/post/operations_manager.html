1. The post

PurposeAccountable for marine operations, vessel management and the safety management system afloat, and for the shoreside support of the fleet. (GSS-COM-ORG-001)
Reports toManaging Director
DepartmentMarine
Stand inAssistant Operations Manager, who stands in for the Operations Manager and then checks that the department's duties are complete.
DirectsAssistant Operations Manager, Crewing Manager, Masters and Skippers, vessel crew and Vessel Engineers (employed and self employed), and the third party that carries out vessel certification, coding and thorough examination of lifting equipment.
Status of the postIn place and filled. The organisation document shows it as one combined post with the Designated Person Ashore; the Company now treats them as two posts.

The title Operations Manager does not appear in merchant shipping law. The Workboat Regulations 2023 put their duties on the owner and the master of each vessel, and the health and safety regulations for ships put theirs on the employer. The owner and the employer are the Company. This post is how the Company carries those duties out ashore, so the core functions below are the owner's and employer's duties written as the work of one post. The Master keeps the authority on board. The Designated Person Ashore monitors what this post delivers.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it. The organisation document gives one list for the combined post. Five of its six lines are placed here and one with the Designated Person Ashore; that split is proposed.

  • Stop or suspend any vessel operation, and direct a vessel to port.
  • Authorise mobilisation and demobilisation.
  • Approve deferral of planned maintenance within a recorded limit, with the reason recorded.
  • Release staff for the internal audit programme.
  • No authority to overrule the Master on board, or to operate a vessel outside its certification.

11. Job advert

Operations Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run our marine department: a workboat fleet working up to 150 miles offshore. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will keep every vessel certificated, maintained, crewed and safe to work, and give our Masters the shore support they need.

What you will do

  • Keep every vessel within its certificate, category and area of operation
  • Lead our move to the Workboat Code Edition 3
  • Run planned maintenance, surveys and defect closure across the fleet
  • Keep vessel risk assessments and safe systems of work current
  • Manage the outside provider for certification and lifting equipment examination
  • Plan and authorise mobilisation and demobilisation
  • Control contractors working on or from our vessels
  • Lead health and safety in the department and respond to vessel emergencies
  • Direct and support Masters, crews, engineers and the crewing team

What you will bring

  • Experience in command of workboats, or as a senior officer, engineer or marine superintendent
  • Detailed knowledge of the Workboat Code Edition 3
  • Working knowledge of merchant shipping health and safety, lifting and accident reporting rules
  • Risk assessment competence
  • Experience of planned maintenance, surveys and certification

It would help if you also have

  • A certificate of competency as master or engineer
  • A health and safety management qualification
  • Contractor management and internal auditor training

We support update training on the Workboat Code, health and safety management training and membership of a relevant professional body.
