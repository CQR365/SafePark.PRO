1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the planning, delivery and safe execution of projects, from enquiry handover through mobilisation to completion and close out, whether the work is delivered from a vessel, from a site, or from both. Taken from the combined post Civils and Project Manager.
Reports toTo confirm. The organisation document has the combined post reporting to the Managing Director. Whether the new post reports to the Managing Director or to the Civils Manager is for the Managing Director to decide.
DepartmentProjects (to confirm)
Stand inTo confirm. Proposed: the Civils Manager.
DirectsThe project team on each project: Site Supervisors, operatives and contractors, and vessel and dive teams through their own managers
Status of the postA new post, not yet in place. It is a qualified project management post, which is not the same as managing a project. Until it is filled its duties are unallocated.

Project Manager is not a title in the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 or in any other regulation read for this draft, and no law requires a project management qualification. Marine civils is construction work under CDM 2015 where it is the construction, alteration, repair, upkeep, maintenance or removal of a structure. The regulations list dock, harbour, sea defence works, river works, pipe or pipeline and cable as structures (regulation 2), so harbour and jetty maintenance will usually be construction work. Chartering a vessel is not in itself construction work. The legal duties on a project sit on the Company as contractor, principal contractor or designer. This post is a professional project management post: it brings a recognised method for planning, control, change and close out, and applies it so that those legal duties are met on each project.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it. The four authority lines are shared between this post and the Civils Manager. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Accept or reject a variation within a recorded value limit, referring anything above it.
  • Proposed: stop or suspend work on a project the post manages.
  • Proposed: no authority to change the scope, method or duty holder position of a project without a recorded review, or to accept a variation above the recorded value limit.

11. Job advert

Project Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

A new post for a qualified project manager. You will plan, deliver and close out our marine, diving and civils projects, from enquiry handover to completion, so that each is safe, on time, within budget and properly recorded.

What you will do

  • Define and plan each project: scope, programme, resources, method, risk and cost
  • Make sure health and safety plans, risk assessments and method statements are in place before work starts
  • Mobilise the team and make sure everyone is inducted
  • Manage the client relationship and report progress
  • Coordinate vessels, divers, site teams and subcontractors
  • Control change: assess every variation for safety and compliance as well as cost
  • Monitor subcontractor performance
  • Close out each project and record the lessons
  • Bring a consistent project management method to the business

What you will bring

  • A recognised project management qualification: APM Project Management Qualification, Chartered Project Professional or PRINCE2 Practitioner
  • Projects delivered as the named project manager, preferably in marine, civils or construction work
  • A current SMSTS certificate
  • Sound working knowledge of the construction design and management regulations
  • Clear written and spoken communication with clients and site teams

It would help if you also have

  • Chartered Project Professional status, or working towards it
  • A CSCS manager card
  • Knowledge of lifting, diving or workboat operations

We support professional development towards chartered status, SMSTS refresher training and membership of the Association for Project Management.
