1. The post

PurposeAccountable for operating the purchase order process, so that goods, equipment and services are ordered from approved suppliers, within authorised limits, and with the certificates and safety information the order calls for.
Reports toThe Financial Controller, who controls procurement authority.
DepartmentFinance (to confirm)
Stand inTo confirm. None is named.
DirectsNo one.
Status of the postIn place and filled. The purchase order role has moved to this post from the Office Manager.

Purchasing is not a role defined in law, and no licence or qualification is needed to hold it. The duties it touches sit on the Company as employer: work equipment must conform to the essential requirements that apply to it, lifting equipment must come with a declaration of conformity or evidence of thorough examination, a substance hazardous to health must be assessed before it is used, protective equipment must be suitable and lawful, and anyone appointing a contractor to construction work must check that the contractor is fit to do it. The point of order is where those duties are met or missed, which is why the post matters to safety as well as to cost.

Authority of the post

Purchase order approval sits with the Financial Controller. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Raise a purchase order on an approved supplier against an authorised request.
  • Refuse to place an order that has no authorised request, or that is above the requester's limit.
  • Hold an order for a new substance, or for work equipment, until the safety data sheet or the certification required has been stated on the order.
  • Quarantine goods that arrive damaged, incorrect or without the certificates ordered.
  • No authority to approve this post's own orders, approve a supplier, change a supplier's bank details, or commit the Company above the limit in the schedule of authorities. That limit is to confirm.

7. The ISO management system: what it needs from this post

The Company is certificated to three ISO standards and purchasing for the marine, diving and civils work is inside the certificated scope, while any purchasing for Silvers is inside the management system but outside the certificated scope. Each standard is described in one sentence, followed by what it needs from this post.

StandardWhat it isWhat it needs from this post
ISO 9001, quality managementThe standard for delivering what the client was promised, the same way every time, and improving how that is done.Orders placed only on approved suppliers, with the requirement and the certificates wanted stated on the order, and goods checked on receipt.
ISO 45001, occupational health and safetyThe standard for preventing injury and ill health at work, with workers involved in how that is done.Safety requirements built into the order, so that equipment arrives with its certificates, substances with their data sheets, and contractors are approved before they are engaged (8.1).
ISO 14001, environmental managementThe standard for knowing how the Company's work affects the environment, controlling it and meeting environmental law.Environmental requirements stated on orders where they apply, for example for fuel, oils, chemicals and waste services.

11. Job advert

Purchasing Officer

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run our purchase order process. Working with the finance team, you will order goods, equipment and services from approved suppliers, within authorised limits, and make sure they arrive with the certificates and safety information our vessels, divers and sites depend on.

What you will do

  • Raise purchase orders against authorised requests
  • Use approved suppliers and put new ones forward for approval
  • State the certificates and safety data sheets needed on each order
  • Check deliveries against the order and file the paperwork
  • Pass certificates and data sheets to our compliance team
  • Hold back damaged, incorrect or uncertificated goods
  • Keep the purchase order list for each job
  • Keep clear purchasing records ready for audit
  • Tell colleagues when their goods have arrived

What you will bring

  • Experience of running a purchase order process from request to invoice match
  • A working knowledge of the certificates and safety information that must come with equipment and substances
  • Awareness of bribery, conflicts of interest and payment fraud
  • Confident use of an accounts or purchase order system and spreadsheets

It would help if you also have

  • A procurement qualification from CIPS
  • Experience of buying for marine, engineering or construction work
  • Basic health and safety awareness training

We support study towards a recognised procurement qualification and the training the post needs.
