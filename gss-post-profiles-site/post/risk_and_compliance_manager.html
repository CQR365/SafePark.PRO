1. The post

PurposeSets the standard for the management system, provides the tools, assures that the standard is met, and reports honestly where it is not. Does not own operational delivery. (GSS-COM-ORG-001)
Reports toManaging Director
DepartmentRisk and compliance. The post owns the management system, health and safety, environmental and information document sets. It assures health and safety in the departments and does not deliver it.
Stand inNone named in the organisation document or the drafts. To confirm. The appointment under regulation 7 is made and withdrawn by the Managing Director and cannot pass to a stand in without a written appointment.
DirectsNo post reports to this post in the organisation document. Internal auditors answer to it for the audit programme. The external health and safety adviser is instructed through it, as an advisory relationship only.
Status of the postIn place and filled. The post holds the written appointment as competent person under regulation 7 of the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 and is the point of contact for external bodies.

The title Risk and Compliance Manager carries no legal definition. Regulation 7 of the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 puts the duty on the employer, which is the Company, to appoint one or more competent persons to assist it. The person appointed assists and advises. The duty to comply stays with the Company and with the managers who control the work. The organisation document states that this post's entries contain no operational delivery, because adding delivery would change the delivery model and affect the audit independence of the function. The core functions below are therefore written as assisting, assuring and reporting, and not as doing the departments' work.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Stop work, anywhere in the business, where there is serious and imminent risk. Exercisable without prior approval and reported immediately afterwards.
  • Place a contractor or supplier on hold pending resolution.
  • Quarantine equipment and withhold it from service.
  • Raise a nonconformity against any department and require a corrective action with a date.
  • Withhold a certificate, accreditation or assurance claim from a bid where it is out of date or outside scope.
  • Determine whether an event is reportable, and report externally to the enforcing authorities and the certification body.
  • Access any site, vessel, record or system for audit or investigation, without notice.
  • Approve issue of a revised document, other than a policy, which is approved by the Managing Director.
  • No authority to direct operational work, to commit expenditure beyond an agreed limit, or to overrule a Master at sea.

11. Job advert

Risk and Compliance Manager

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will maintain our integrated management system and hold the appointment as our competent person for health and safety. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will set the standard, give managers the tools, check it is met and report honestly where it is not.

What you will do

  • Advise the Managing Director and managers on health, safety and environmental law
  • Keep the management system and its documents controlled and up to date
  • Maintain the legal register and the corporate risk register
  • Design and run the internal audit programme across three ISO standards
  • Investigate incidents, decide what is reportable and report it
  • Check that corrective actions deal with the cause and stay closed
  • Manage our certifications, accreditations and their renewals
  • Supply current assurance evidence for tenders
  • Act as our point of contact with regulators and the certification body

What you will bring

  • A diploma level qualification in occupational health and safety, or equivalent
  • Chartered or graduate level membership of a relevant professional institution
  • Lead auditor training for ISO 9001, ISO 14001 or ISO 45001
  • Accredited training in incident investigation and root cause analysis
  • A risk management qualification or equivalent experience

It would help if you also have

  • An environmental management qualification
  • Knowledge of marine, diving or construction regulation
  • Experience of client assurance schemes and business continuity planning

We support the continuing professional development that goes with professional membership and auditor training.
