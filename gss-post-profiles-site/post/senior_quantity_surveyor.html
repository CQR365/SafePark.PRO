The title Senior Quantity Surveyor carries no definition in the law read for this draft, and no legal requirement to hold a qualification or membership was found. Quantity surveying is a profession whose recognised body is the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS). The legal duties that bear on the work sit on the Company: the payment rules for construction contracts, the laws on bribery and on collusion in tendering, and the duty to take on only work it can deliver safely. Where the post holder is a member of RICS, the RICS Rules of Conduct also bind the individual.

Authority of the post

The list below is for the Managing Director to confirm.

  • Proposed: open a job file and run the tender review for any enquiry.
  • Proposed: issue valuations, applications for payment and payment notices on live contracts.
  • Proposed: price and recommend variations, for acceptance by the Commercial Manager or the manager who holds the value limit.
  • Proposed: no authority to submit a bid, accept contract terms or accept a variation without the recorded review, or to claim an accreditation or capability outside its recorded scope.

11. Job advert

Senior Quantity Surveyor

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run our tender and contract review process and look after the commercial side of our marine, diving and civils contracts, from first enquiry to final account, keeping a complete and auditable file for every job.

What you will do

  • Review enquiries and open a job file for each tender
  • Measure, price and prepare tender returns by the client's date
  • Carry out and record tender and contract reviews
  • Check that the certificates, accreditations and insurance we rely on cover the work
  • Value work, apply for payment and manage payment notices
  • Price, record and agree variations
  • Check subcontractor competence and insurance before orders are placed
  • Gather customer feedback, handle complaints and carry out post contract reviews
  • Keep our pre qualification scheme entries current

What you will bring

  • Substantial experience as a quantity surveyor on marine, civils or construction contracts
  • A degree or equivalent qualification in quantity surveying or a closely related subject
  • Working knowledge of construction contract payment rules and standard forms of contract
  • Accurate, orderly record keeping to an audited standard
  • Clear communication with clients, subcontractors and site teams

It would help if you also have

  • Chartered membership of RICS, or working towards it
  • Working knowledge of construction health and safety duties
  • Experience of pre qualification schemes

We support membership of RICS or a similar body and the continuing professional development it requires.
