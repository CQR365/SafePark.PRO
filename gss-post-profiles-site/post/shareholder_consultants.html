1. The post

PurposeGive the Managing Director advice and agreed services under a written consultancy engagement, without holding any office or any management authority in the Company. (Proposed)
Reports toManaging Director, for the services engaged. As shareholders they act separately, as members of the Company, through shareholders' resolutions.
DepartmentNone. Outside the management structure.
Stand inNot applicable. One shareholder consultant is at present standing in as Silvers Yard Manager and signing off there until that post is filled.
DirectsNo one, unless a written authority from the Managing Director gives a defined line. To confirm for the Silvers Yard stand in.
Status of the postTwo shareholders engaged as consultants. Not directors and not employees. No written engagement was seen for either.

"Shareholder consultant" has no meaning in law. A shareholder has rights as a member of the Company and no authority to manage it by reason of the shares. A consultant has only the authority the engagement gives. The law looks at what a person does, not at the title. In the Companies Act 2006, "director" includes any person occupying the position of director, by whatever name called (section 250), and a shadow director is a person in accordance with whose directions or instructions the directors are accustomed to act (section 251). Advice given in a professional capacity does not, on its own, make the adviser a shadow director (section 251(2)). A person who is not appointed but acts as a director is commonly called a de facto director. A person found to be either can carry a director's duties and liabilities. With a sole director and two shareholders who work in the business, the line between advising and directing needs to be written down and kept.

Authority of the post

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 has no entry for the shareholder consultants, so the list below is proposed. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by a consultant.

  • Advise the Managing Director on the matters set out in the written engagement.
  • Act for the Company only where a written authority from the Managing Director names the task, the limit and the period.
  • For the Silvers Yard stand in: what may be signed off, to what value and until when, to be set out in writing by the Managing Director.
  • No authority to commit the Company, sign contracts, engage or dismiss anyone, change a procedure, or instruct staff outside a line given in writing. No authority to take any decision that belongs to the Managing Director.
