1. The post

PurposeAccountable for supervising the yard's work day to day, for standing in for the Silvers Yard Manager, and for checking that the yard's duties are complete.
Reports toSilvers Yard Manager (to confirm). While that post is vacant, the shareholder consultant who is standing in.
DepartmentSilvers Marine: the Company's boatyard and marina at Silverhills, Rosneath
Stand inTo confirm. No post is named to stand in for the supervisor.
DirectsSilvers Yard General Duties (a working title) on the day's work. Customers' contractors on site, under the Yard Manager's control.
Status of the postWorking title, not yet confirmed.

The title carries no legal definition. The remit is the Yard Manager's: the assistant stands in for the manager, then checks that the yard's duties are complete. The legal duties stay with the Company, and the regulations expect anyone who supervises lifting, work equipment or work at height to be trained and competent for it. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is never carried by a deputy.

Authority of the post

The list mirrors the Yard Manager's authority for use when standing in.

  • Stop any yard or marina operation that is unsafe, at any time.
  • Refuse a lift, or stop one in progress.
  • When standing in for the Yard Manager: close an area of the site, and refuse a customer or contractor access on safety grounds.
  • No authority to allow a lift outside its plan or with equipment outside its thorough examination date, to change a lifting plan made by the competent person unless appointed to plan lifts, or to use any authority reserved to the Managing Director.

11. Job advert

Assistant Yard Supervisor, Silvers Marine

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will supervise the day to day work of our boatyard and marina on the Gare Loch and stand in for the Yard Manager. You will lead the team on lifting, launching, storage ashore and berthing, and keep the work safe for staff and customers.

What you will do

  • Supervise boat lifting, launching and moves with the boat hoist, cranes and telehandler
  • Operate yard plant and check it and its lifting gear before use
  • Set boats safely ashore on cradles and props, and keep the yard tidy and safe to walk through
  • Brief and check contractors and customers working on their boats
  • Help customers on the pontoons and in the yard
  • Apply our controls for fuel, gas, coatings, waste and hull washing
  • Stand in for the Yard Manager, and check that inspections and records are up to date
  • Report defects, near misses and incidents straight away

What you will bring

  • Experience of boat lifting, launching and storage ashore in a working boatyard or marina
  • Operator training on a boat hoist, and on a lift truck or telehandler
  • Slinger and signaller training, and the ability to supervise routine lifts
  • A current first aid at work certificate, or willingness to gain one on joining
  • A calm, practical way with customers and a team

It would help if you also have

  • A powerboat handling certificate
  • A health and safety qualification for supervisors
  • Marina operative or marina management training

We provide plant and lifting refresher training and first aid, and support marina training towards management.
