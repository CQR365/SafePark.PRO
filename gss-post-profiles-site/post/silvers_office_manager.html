1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the Silvers Marine office: customer contracts and berthing records, customer accounts and payments, and the customer information the yard needs to do its work.
Reports toThe Silvers Yard Manager for the office and customers, and the Financial Controller for the accounts.
DepartmentSilvers Marine: the Company's boatyard and marina at Silverhills, Rosneath
Stand inTo confirm. The Accounts Assistant post also does the Silvers accounts and could cover that part.
DirectsTo confirm. No staff are shown for this post in the data.
Status of the postIn place and filled. Title to confirm.

The title carries no legal definition and no registration attaches to it. The legal duties the post touches fall on the Company: keeping accounting records, handling customers' personal data lawfully, and offering consumers fair contract terms. This post is how the Company carries them out for Silvers customers. The draft task list treats the post as an accounts post, while the title says office manager; the core functions below cover both, so that the Managing Director can decide what the post is.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it.

  • Issue berthing, storage and yard work agreements on the Company's standard terms and prices.
  • Raise sales invoices, post receipts and send customer statements for Silvers.
  • Chase overdue accounts, and refer a disputed or long overdue account to the Financial Controller.
  • Refuse to release customer personal data without a lawful reason.
  • No authority to change contract terms or prices, to write off a debt, or to both authorise and pay the same purchase invoice without a second person. Limits to be set by the Financial Controller.

11. Job advert

Office Manager, Silvers Marine

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run the office of our boatyard and marina on the Gare Loch. You will look after customer contracts, berthing and storage records, invoicing and customer accounts, and make sure the yard team has the customer information it needs.

What you will do

  • Issue and renew berthing, storage and yard work agreements
  • Keep the berth and storage register and customer records accurate
  • Raise sales invoices, post receipts and send customer statements
  • Post purchase invoices and prepare supplier payments
  • Reconcile the bank and the purchase ledger, and control credit
  • Help with job costings and with information for the auditors
  • Keep customer personal data safe and used only as it should be
  • Tell the yard which contractors customers are bringing on site
  • Be the first point of contact for customers, in person, by phone and by email

What you will bring

  • Experience of running a customer facing office, with contracts, invoicing and customer accounts
  • Bookkeeping experience: sales and purchase ledgers, bank reconciliation and credit control
  • A working knowledge of data protection as it applies to customer records
  • Confident use of accounts and office software

It would help if you also have

  • A bookkeeping or accounting qualification
  • Experience of berthing or marina administration
  • A first aid certificate, or fire warden training

We support bookkeeping and accounting study, data protection training and marina administration training.
