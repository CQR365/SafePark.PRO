1. The post

PurposeAccountable for carrying out the yard and marina work as instructed and trained: lifting, launching and storing boats, moving plant and gear, keeping the site safe and clean, and helping customers.
Reports toSilvers Yard Manager, through the Silvers Assistant Yard Supervisor (to confirm)
DepartmentSilvers Marine: the Company's boatyard and marina at Silverhills, Rosneath
Stand inNot applicable. Cover is arranged within the yard team.
DirectsNo one
Status of the postWorking title, not yet confirmed.

The title carries no legal definition and no licence is needed to work in a boatyard. The legal duties for the yard fall on the Company. The law puts three plain duties on every employee: to take reasonable care, to use equipment as trained and instructed, and to report danger. The Company in turn must train the post holder before they use any plant. The description of the occupation below follows the recognised marina and boatyard operative role.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it.

  • Stop own work, and call a stop to any lift or job, when something is unsafe.
  • Refuse to use plant or lifting gear that is defective, out of test, or that the post holder has not been trained and authorised to use.
  • Ask anyone to leave a lifting area or another area of danger.
  • No authority to operate plant without training and authorisation, to change a lifting plan, to move or adjust the supports of a boat ashore without instruction, or to admit contractors to the site.

11. Job advert

Yard Operative (General Duties), Silvers Marine

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will be part of the small team that runs our boatyard and marina on the Gare Loch. The work is practical and outdoors: lifting, launching and storing boats, keeping the yard and pontoons safe and tidy, and helping our customers.

What you will do

  • Help lift, launch, move and store boats ashore as part of a trained team
  • Set up cradles and props under instruction
  • Pressure wash hulls and keep washings and waste out of the water
  • Check plant and lifting gear before use and report defects
  • Operate yard plant once trained and authorised
  • Help customers on the pontoons and in the yard
  • Keep the yard, pontoons and workshops clean, tidy and safe
  • Follow the yard rules and report anything unsafe straight away

What you will bring

  • Practical experience of yard, marine, plant or similar outdoor manual work
  • Willingness and fitness to be trained on our plant before you use it
  • A careful attitude to safety around lifting, heights and water
  • A friendly and helpful way with customers

It would help if you also have

  • Lift truck, telehandler or boat hoist operator training, or slinger and signaller training
  • A first aid certificate
  • A powerboat handling certificate, or marina operative training

We train you on our plant before you use it, and support plant, lifting, first aid and marina operative training as you progress.
