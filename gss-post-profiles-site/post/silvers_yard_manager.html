1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the boatyard and marina operation, its plant, its people and the safety of customers and members of the public on site.
Reports toManaging Director
DepartmentSilvers Marine: the Company's boatyard and marina at Silverhills, Rosneath. Silvers Marine is a trading name and site of the Company, not a separate company.
Stand inSilvers Assistant Yard Supervisor (a working title, not yet confirmed), who stands in for the Yard Manager and then checks that the yard's duties are complete.
DirectsSilvers Assistant Yard Supervisor and Silvers Yard General Duties (both working titles). Controls customers, visitors and customers' own contractors while they are on site. The reporting line of the Silvers Office Manager is to confirm.
Status of the postVacant and being advertised. A shareholder consultant is standing in and signing off until it is filled.

No law defines the title Yard Manager or marina manager, and no licence or registration attaches to it. The duties come from general health and safety, fire and environmental law, and they fall on the Company as employer and as the person in control of the premises. This post is how the Company carries them out at Silvers, so the core functions below are the Company's duties written as the work of one post. The organisation document writes its entry for a post it calls Silvers General Manager; this profile carries that entry on the working assumption that the Yard Manager is now the senior Silvers post.

Authority of the post

What the post may decide without further approval, and the limit on it, as set out in GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 for the post it calls Silvers General Manager, carried here on the working assumption. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • Stop or suspend any yard or marina operation.
  • Refuse a lift, or stop one in progress.
  • Close an area of the site to staff, customers or the public.
  • Refuse a customer or contractor access on safety grounds.
  • The organisation document states no limit for this post. Proposed limit: no authority to allow a lift outside its plan, or with equipment that is outside its thorough examination date.

11. Job advert

Yard Manager, Silvers Marine

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will run Silvers Marine, our boatyard and marina on the Gare Loch. Reporting to the Managing Director, you will lead a small yard team, look after customers and their boats, and make sure every lift, launch and yard job is planned and done safely.

What you will do

  • Plan and supervise boat lifting, launching and storage ashore with the boat hoist, cranes and telehandler
  • Keep yard plant and lifting equipment maintained, examined on time and recorded
  • Lead health and safety on a working site that customers and the public also use
  • Control contractors and customers' own contractors working on boats in the yard
  • Manage berthing, pontoons, moorings and site services, including electrical and fire safety checks
  • Look after fuel, gas, coatings and waste so that nothing reaches the water
  • Lead, train and develop the yard team
  • Give customers a clear, friendly and reliable service, and schedule the yard's work
  • Keep the records, report incidents and work with our compliance team on audits

What you will bring

  • Experience of managing or supervising a working boatyard or marina, including boat lifting and storage ashore
  • Proven competence to plan and supervise lifting operations with boat hoists and cranes
  • A working knowledge of the lifting and work equipment regulations and of statutory inspections
  • Competence in hazardous substance assessments for coatings and preparation, and a clear grasp of the duties owed to customers and the public on site
  • Experience of managing people, plant and records to an audited standard

It would help if you also have

  • A health and safety management qualification, and a first aid certificate
  • Operator training for a boat hoist, lift truck or telehandler, and a powerboat handling certificate
  • The Intermediate Marina Manager course or progress towards Certified Marina Manager

We support recognised marina management training, lifting and plant refresher training, first aid, and membership of a relevant professional body.
