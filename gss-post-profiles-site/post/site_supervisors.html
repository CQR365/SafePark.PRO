1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the safe, compliant and recorded running of the work at the site or location ashore placed in the post holder's charge, and for the supervision of the people and contractors working there. (Proposed)
Reports toTo confirm. The organisation document has no entry for this post. Proposed: the Civils Manager, or the Project Manager for the project once that post is filled.
DepartmentCivils and projects (to confirm). The drafts also use the word supervisor for the person in charge of a location in the yard or workshop.
Stand inTo confirm. No stand in is named in the drafts.
DirectsOperatives, self employed workers and contractors at the site or location in the post holder's charge
Status of the postTo confirm. The post is not in the list of posts in place and has no entry in the organisation document. The drafts give 86 tasks to "supervisors", "the supervisor in charge of the location" and the "site contact".

The law does not define a site supervisor and gives the title no duties of its own. It puts the duty to provide supervision on the employer and, on construction work, on the contractor, which is the Company. A supervisor is how that duty is delivered on the ground. As an employee the supervisor also carries the general duties every employee has: to take reasonable care and to cooperate with the employer. Marine civils is construction work under CDM 2015 where it is the construction, alteration, repair, upkeep, maintenance or removal of a structure. The regulations list dock, harbour, sea defence works, river works, pipe or pipeline and cable as structures (regulation 2), so harbour and jetty maintenance will usually be construction work. Chartering a vessel is not in itself construction work.

Authority of the post

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 has no entry for this post, so it sets no authority. The list below is proposed, drawn from what the draft procedures already have a supervisor decide, for the Managing Director to confirm.

  • Proposed: stop any job at the site or location in the post holder's charge, and require it to be made safe.
  • Proposed: refuse entry to the site, and refuse any person, plant or equipment that is not inducted, authorised or in date.
  • Proposed: adjust or stop a task on grounds of weather, heat or cold.
  • Proposed: no authority to change a method statement, lifting plan or permit condition, to restart stopped work without the confirmation the stop the job procedure requires, or to accept a variation to the work.

11. Job advert

Site Supervisor

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will take charge of our work on site: marine civils, harbour and jetty maintenance, often on or beside the water. You will supervise our team and contractors so that the job is done safely, to the plan and with the records to show it.

What you will do

  • Induct everyone before they start and brief the team each day
  • Supervise the work to the method statement, risk assessment and permits
  • Check that people are trained and that plant and lifting equipment is in date before use
  • Supervise lifting operations and stop any that depart from the plan
  • Keep the site secure, tidy and provided with welfare
  • Keep rescue equipment ready and lead the response to any emergency or spill
  • Supervise contractors day to day
  • Inspect the workplace and act on what you find
  • Record and report accidents, near misses and stopped jobs straight away

What you will bring

  • Experience of civils, marine or similar site work
  • A current SSSTS certificate
  • Training in the plant and lifting operations you will supervise
  • A first aid certificate, and confidence working on or near water
  • The confidence to stop a job that is not safe

It would help if you also have

  • An SMSTS certificate
  • A CSCS supervisor card
  • Permit, slinger and signaller training

We support refresher training for site safety certificates, first aid and plant tickets, and progression to site management training.
