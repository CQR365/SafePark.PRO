1. The post

PurposeAccountable to the master for carrying out the deck, watch, safety and housekeeping work of the vessel safely and as briefed, and for holding the certificates and medical their place on board needs. (Proposed)
Reports toThe Master or Skipper of the vessel
DepartmentMarine operations (vessels)
Stand inNot applicable to a team post. Crew are assigned to a vessel by Crewing.
DirectsNone
Status of the postIn place. The titles of the crew posts (for example mate, deckhand, safety boat operator, cook) are not defined, and the post has no entry in the organisation document. How many crew are employed and how many self employed is to confirm.

The Merchant Shipping Act 1995 (section 313) calls every person employed or engaged on board, other than the master and a pilot, a seaman. The Maritime Labour Convention regulations use "seafarer" and include anyone who works on board, employed or self employed. Titles such as mate and deckhand carry no legal definition on a small workboat. What the law fixes is the duty of every worker on board, the medical certificate, the hours of rest, and the certificate needed by anyone put in charge of a navigational watch. The duties to man the vessel safely are on the owner and the master, and the crew are how they are met.

Authority of the post

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 has no entry for this post, so the list below is proposed. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post.

  • Stop their own work, or work they are involved in, where they believe it is unsafe (the universal authority in the organisation document, Part 7).
  • Take defective equipment out of use and make it safe (the organisation document says anyone may do so, MST-R05).
  • Decline to take over a watch if not satisfied, with the master called (draft checklist CHK 19).
  • Raise a complaint through the on board complaints procedure without being disadvantaged.
  • Limit: no authority over the navigation, loading or operation of the vessel except as the master delegates, and no authority to alter a plan, a permit or the master's standing orders.

10. Gaps carried to the risk register

Each gap carries a first pass rating for the Risk and Compliance Manager to confirm or change. Rating = likelihood (L) x severity (S), each 1 to 5: LOW 1 to 6, MEDIUM 8 to 10, HIGH 12 to 25.

11. Job advert

Workboat Deckhand

Gareloch Support Services (Plant) Ltd, Rosneath, Argyll and Bute

Gareloch Support Services is a marine and diving support company working from the Gare Loch, with its own workboat fleet, a commercial diving team, a marine civils capability and a boatyard and marina.

The role

You will join the crew of one of our workboats, working up to 150 miles offshore in support of marine construction, diving, towing and survey. Answering to the skipper, you will do the deck work safely and help keep the vessel clean, maintained and ready.

What you will do

  • Handle ropes and wires for berthing, mooring, towing and anchor work
  • Sling loads and help with lifting and equipment handling
  • Keep a deck watch and lookout, and steer when the skipper authorises it
  • Keep the vessel clean, tidy and maintained
  • Take your turn in the galley and follow the food hygiene rules
  • Wear your lifejacket and protective equipment and look after them
  • Take part in briefings, drills and safety meetings
  • Report every accident, near miss and defect to the skipper that day
  • Tell the skipper if you are too tired to work safely

What you will bring

  • A valid seafarer medical: ENG 1 for work beyond 60 miles, or ML5 for work closer in
  • A sea survival certificate
  • Elementary first aid and fire fighting certificates
  • Some experience on deck on a workboat, tug, fishing vessel or similar
  • A Level 2 food safety certificate, or willingness to gain one before handling food

It would help if you also have

  • A certificate of competency or watchkeeping experience
  • A radio operator's certificate or an Approved Engine Course certificate
  • Slinger, signaller or powerboat training

We support refresher training and progression towards watchkeeping, engineering and skipper certificates.
