How SafeMiss is calculated:
Completed maintenance tasks rated high, urgent, or critical are counted as proactive safety catches. Each catch represents a hazard identified and resolved before it could cause injury.

SafeSystems.PRO model: Category base cost × Priority multiplier (critical 3×, urgent 2.5×, high 2×).
Category costs: Structural/Building £4,000 · Gas £3,500 · Fire/Safety £3,000 · Electrical £2,500 · Heating £2,000 · Water £2,000 · Playground £2,000 · Plumbing £1,500 · Equipment £1,200 · General/Other £800 · Grounds £600 · Cleaning £400

HSE benchmark: The UK Health & Safety Executive estimates each non-fatal workplace injury (7+ days absence) costs employers £7,500 on average, or £44,300 total cost to society (2024 prices). Our model is intentionally conservative, estimating disruption and repair costs rather than full injury costs. If any catch prevented a RIDDOR-reportable injury, the true saving would be significantly higher.
