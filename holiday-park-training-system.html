Legislative Framework & Compliance

Legal requirements and regulatory compliance framework

Legal Compliance Overview

This documentation suite ensures compliance with all relevant UK health and safety legislation. Understanding these legal requirements is fundamental to safe operations and legal compliance.

Primary Legislation

Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974

The primary piece of legislation covering occupational health and safety in the United Kingdom.

Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999

Sets out requirements for risk assessment, health surveillance, and safety management systems.

Control of Substances Hazardous to Health Regulations 2002 (COSHH)

Requires employers to control exposure to hazardous substances to prevent ill health.

Equipment and Workplace Safety

Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations 1992

Sets requirements for provision, use, and maintenance of personal protective equipment.

Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992

Requires assessment and control of manual handling risks to prevent musculoskeletal injuries.

Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations 1998 (PUWER)

Ensures work equipment is suitable, properly maintained, and used safely.

Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998 (LOLER)

Requires thorough examination and safe use of lifting equipment.

Water Safety and Quality

Water Supply (Water Quality) Regulations 2016

Sets standards for water quality and safety in supply systems.

Private Water Supplies Regulations 2016

Regulates private water supplies including risk assessment requirements.

Legionnaires' Disease - The Control of Legionella Bacteria in Water Systems L8

HSE Approved Code of Practice for preventing legionella in water systems.

Fire and Electrical Safety

Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005

Places duties on responsible persons to carry out fire risk assessments.

Electricity at Work Regulations 1989

Requires electrical systems to be maintained to prevent danger.

Construction and Environmental

Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015

Applies to construction work including maintenance and repair activities.

Environmental Protection Act 1990

Controls environmental pollution and waste management.

Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013 (RIDDOR)

Requires reporting of serious workplace accidents, occupational diseases and dangerous occurrences.
