Holiday Park General Safety (HPGS) Checklist System

Comprehensive safety inspection and compliance management for holiday park operations

System Overview

This comprehensive safety checklist system ensures your holiday park maintains the highest standards of safety across all operational areas. The system is divided into three inspection frequencies:

  • Monthly Inspections: Critical safety systems requiring regular monitoring
  • 6-Monthly Inspections: Operational systems and compliance requirements
  • Annual Inspections: Comprehensive reviews and strategic assessments

Each section includes detailed checklists, comment areas for maintenance requirements, and mandatory acknowledgment procedures. All data is automatically saved to Firebase for compliance tracking and reporting.

Current Site: Oban Holiday Park
