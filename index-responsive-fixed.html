Near Miss Reporting System - USP Module

safemiss.pro - Near Miss Reporting

Comprehensive near miss reporting system designed to capture and analyze potential incidents before they become accidents. The USP (Unique Selling Proposition) module of the SafePark.PRO suite.

  • Team-reported near miss incidents with detailed categorization
  • Guest-reported safety concerns with priority flagging
  • Trend analysis and risk assessment algorithms
  • Automated alert system for recurring near miss patterns
  • Integration with management dashboards and reporting systems
  • Photo and video evidence capture capabilities
  • Real-time notifications to safety managers
  • Preventive action tracking and implementation monitoring
  • Anonymous reporting options to encourage disclosure
  • Integration with incident reporting for comprehensive safety tracking

Regulatory Compliance

Health and Safety at Work Act 1974: Duty to ensure workplace safety and prevent incidents
Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999: Risk assessment and prevention requirements
Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (RIDDOR): Near miss reporting requirements
