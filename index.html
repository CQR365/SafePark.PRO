STLL Requirements & Documentation

Safety Certificates

  • Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) - Valid 5 years
  • Gas Safety Certificate - Annual requirement
  • Portable Appliance Testing (PAT) - Annual
  • Fire Safety Checklist - Scottish Fire & Rescue Service
  • Legionella Risk Assessment

Property Documentation

  • Floor plans (scaled A4 format)
  • Buildings insurance with £2M public liability
  • Property ownership/consent documentation
  • Planning permission (if required)
  • Public display notice compliance

Application Process

  • Fit and proper person assessment
  • Local authority consultation (28 days)
  • Police Scotland background check
  • Fire service property inspection
  • Processing time: 6-12 months

Licence Categories

  • Home Sharing: Living in property while letting
  • Home Letting: Letting while temporarily absent
  • Secondary Letting: Second homes/investment properties
  • Provisional: Properties under construction

⚠️ Important Legal Requirements

  • Operating without a licence is a criminal offence - fines up to £2,500
  • Cannot take bookings until licence granted
  • Licence valid for 3 years, then renewal required
  • Mandatory conditions apply to all licence types
  • Local planning permission may be required
