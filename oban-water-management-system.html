Risk Assessments

Comprehensive risk management for PWS operations

HIGH RISK: Source Water Contamination

Risk: Contamination of pond source water from agricultural runoff, wildlife, or extreme weather events.

Mitigation: Daily visual inspections, enhanced treatment protocols, boil water notices when required, alternative water supply available.

Review Date: Monthly

HIGH RISK: UV System Failure

Risk: Dual UV system failure resulting in inadequate disinfection of treated water supply.

Mitigation: Redundant UV systems, regular maintenance, UV intensity monitoring, emergency protocols, guest notification systems.

Review Date: Quarterly

MEDIUM RISK: Filter System Blockage

Risk: Turbidex filtration system blockage reducing treatment efficacy and flow rates.

Mitigation: Regular backwashing, pressure monitoring, spare filter media, maintenance schedule adherence.

Review Date: Monthly

CRITICAL RISK: Storage Tank Contamination - NEVER CLEANED

Risk: IMMEDIATE CRITICAL RISK - Storage tanks have NEVER been deep cleaned or disinfected. Potential for biofilm formation, pathogen growth, and severe contamination. This represents the highest risk to guest health and safety.

Mitigation: URGENT - Schedule immediate professional tank cleaning service. Implement enhanced chlorination if possible. Maintain boil water notice until tanks properly cleaned and tested. Arrange temporary alternative water supply during cleaning.

Review Date: IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED

LOW RISK: Power Supply Interruption

Risk: Power outage affecting pumps and UV systems, interrupting water supply to guests.

Mitigation: Backup generator available, storage capacity for several hours, guest communication procedures.

Review Date: Annually

LOW RISK: Seasonal Demand Fluctuation

Risk: Seasonal variation in guest numbers affecting water demand and system operation patterns.

Mitigation: Flexible operating procedures, capacity planning, off-season maintenance scheduling.

Review Date: Annually

Safe Working Procedures

Standard operating procedures for PWS maintenance

Daily Operating Procedures

Morning Checks (Before 10:00)

  1. Visual inspection of pond source water for debris, discoloration, or algae
  2. Check UV system pressures (System A: 2.4 bar, System B: 2.1 bar)
  3. Verify UV lamp operation lights are functioning
  4. Check storage tank water levels (target: 80-90% capacity)
  5. Test a sample tap for flow rate and visual water quality
  6. Confirm guest notification boards are in place and legible
  7. Record all observations in daily log

Evening Checks (After 18:00)

  1. Re-check UV system pressures and alarm status
  2. Monitor storage tank levels after day's usage
  3. Secure all access points with proper locks
  4. Check emergency contact information is accessible
  5. Complete daily record entry

Weekly Maintenance Procedures

Every Monday (or next working day)

  1. Pond Maintenance:
    • Remove debris from intake screen
    • Clean intake area of organic matter
    • Check intake structure integrity
    • Document debris levels and water clarity
  2. Filtration System:
    • Perform Turbidex backwash cycle (20-30 minutes)
    • Check pressure differential across filters
    • Inspect filter housings for leaks
    • Test system flow rates
  3. UV Systems:
    • Clean quartz sleeves with appropriate cleaner
    • Check UV intensity readings
    • Inspect electrical connections
    • Test alarm systems
  4. Documentation:
    • Complete weekly maintenance log
    • Update maintenance schedule board
    • File any defect reports

Emergency Procedures

UV System Failure Protocol

  1. Immediate Actions (within 15 minutes):
    • Activate boil water notice for all guests
    • Post clear signage at all taps and guest areas
    • Announce over PA system if available
    • Switch to bottled water supply for drinking
  2. Within 1 Hour:
    • Contact Highland Holidays management
    • Notify Argyll & Bute Council Environmental Health
    • Contact maintenance contractor
    • Document incident in emergency log
  3. Ongoing:
    • Provide guest information updates
    • Maintain bottled water supply
    • Monitor repair progress
    • Continue boil water notice until system restored and tested

Source Water Contamination Protocol

  1. Immediate Actions:
    • Stop intake if contamination is severe
    • Activate boil water notice
    • Switch to stored water only
    • Assess contamination source
  2. Investigation:
    • Inspect upstream of intake
    • Check for agricultural activity
    • Look for dead animals or major pollution
    • Take photographs for evidence
  3. Recovery:
    • Arrange water quality testing
    • Clean intake and filters if needed
    • Resume operation only when safe
    • Maintain enhanced monitoring

🚨 URGENT: Storage Tank Deep Cleaning Procedures

CRITICAL COMPLIANCE ISSUE - NEVER PERFORMED

IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED - PROFESSIONAL TANK CLEANING

Storage tanks have NEVER been deep cleaned. This represents a critical health risk and compliance failure.

Professional Contractor Requirements:

  1. Certification: UKAS accredited water tank cleaning contractor
  2. Insurance: Minimum £2M public liability insurance
  3. Standards: Must follow BS 8558:2015 cleaning procedures
  4. Documentation: Full cleaning certificate and photographic evidence
  5. Testing: Post-cleaning water quality analysis required

Cleaning Process (Professional Only):

  1. Pre-cleaning Assessment:
    • Complete drainage and inspection
    • Photographic documentation of current condition
    • Risk assessment for confined space entry
    • Health and safety briefing
  2. Physical Cleaning:
    • High-pressure washing of all surfaces
    • Mechanical removal of biofilm and sediment
    • Cleaning of tank fittings and pipework
    • Inspection of tank structural integrity
  3. Disinfection:
    • Application of 50mg/L sodium hypochlorite solution
    • Minimum 4-hour contact time
    • Complete system flush with treated water
    • Chlorine residual testing
  4. Post-Cleaning Validation:
    • Water sampling from multiple points
    • Microbiological analysis (24-48 hour results)
    • Chemical analysis for cleaning residues
    • Final cleaning certificate issued

Emergency Measures Until Cleaning:

  • Maintain boil water notice - absolutely critical
  • Enhance chlorination if technically possible
  • Increase monitoring frequency - daily visual checks
  • Guest communication - ensure bottled water availability
  • Environmental Health notification - inform of action plan

Safety Requirements

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Safety boots with good grip (pond area can be slippery)
  • High-visibility vest for site work
  • Waterproof gloves for handling equipment
  • Eye protection when using cleaning chemicals
  • First aid kit accessible at all times

Lone Working Protocol

  • Always inform someone of maintenance schedule
  • Carry mobile phone with emergency contacts
  • Use buddy system for complex maintenance
  • Never enter confined spaces alone
  • Complete check-in protocol after work

Chemical Handling

  • Store all chemicals in locked, ventilated area
  • Follow manufacturer's safety data sheets
  • Never mix different cleaning products
  • Ensure adequate ventilation when using
  • Have eye wash station available
  • Dispose of waste according to regulations
