Daily Safety Inspection Checklist

Complete all 8 mandatory checks. Mark any issues found and they'll automatically create maintenance tasks.

1. Visual Inspection - Equipment & Infrastructure
Check for obvious damage, wear, loose bolts, cracks, or structural issues
2. Surface & Fall Zone Inspection
Check impact-absorbing surfaces, drainage, level areas, debris removal
3. Equipment Function & Safety Check
Test moving parts, check chains/cables, verify all equipment operates safely
4. Perimeter Fencing & Boundaries
Inspect fence integrity, post stability, no protruding elements or damage
5. Gates & Access Control
Verify gate operation, self-closing mechanisms, latch functionality
6. Safety Signage & Information
Check age group signs, safety rules, emergency contacts, compliance signage
7. Environmental Hazards & Cleanliness
Look for glass, litter, animal waste, standing water, vegetation overgrowth
8. Accessibility & General Condition
Check pathways, accessibility compliance, overall site presentation
