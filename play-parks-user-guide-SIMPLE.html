Play Parks Safety System

Simple User Guide - How to Use the System

Getting Started

This guide will help you understand how to use the Play Parks Safety System to manage daily inspections and keep your play areas safe.

What You Need

  • Your unique access code (provided by your manager)
  • A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
  • 5-10 minutes for each daily inspection
Important: Your access code determines which holiday parks you can inspect. Keep this code secure and do not share it with others.

Logging Into the System

  1. 1 Open your web browser and go to the SafePark.PRO website
  2. 2 Enter your access code in the login box
  3. 3 Click "Access System"
[Screenshot would show: Login screen with access code entry field and "Access System" button]
Quick Tip: The system will remember your login for 60 minutes, so you won't need to enter your code again during that time.

Understanding Your Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard showing:

  • Today's Inspection Status: Which parks still need inspection
  • Recent Activity: Your completed inspections
  • Quick Actions: Buttons to start new inspections
[Screenshot would show: Dashboard with inspection status cards showing "Holiday Park 1 - COMPLETE", "Holiday Park 2 - PENDING", etc.]
Status Color Meaning What to Do
Green - COMPLETE Inspection finished for today Nothing required
Yellow - PENDING Inspection still needed Click to start inspection
Red - OVERDUE Inspection was missed Complete immediately

Starting a Daily Inspection

  1. 1 From your dashboard, click on a holiday park that shows "PENDING"
  2. 2 Click "Start Daily Inspection"
  3. 3 You'll see the inspection checklist appear
[Screenshot would show: Park selection dropdown and "Start Daily Inspection" button]

Completing the Safety Checklist

The inspection has 8 important safety checks you must complete:

Check What to Look For
General Visual Inspection Overall condition, no obvious damage or hazards
Surface Safety Check No trip hazards, clean surfaces, proper depth
Equipment Integrity All equipment secure, no loose bolts or damage
Perimeter Fencing Check Fence intact, no gaps, panels secure
Gate Operation Check Gates close properly, latches work
Safety Signage Check All signs present, readable, emergency contacts visible
Environmental Hazards No vandalism, litter cleared, vegetation managed
Access Routes Clear Pathways clear, no obstructions
[Screenshot would show: Inspection checklist with 8 checkboxes and descriptions]

How to Complete Each Check

  1. 1 Walk through the play area and check each item carefully
  2. 2 Tick the checkbox if everything is safe and in good condition
  3. 3 If you find a problem, note it in the "Issues" section
  4. 4 Add any additional notes in the "Comments" box

Reporting Issues or Problems

If you find any safety concerns during your inspection:

  1. 1 Do not tick the checkbox for that item
  2. 2 Describe the problem in the "Issues Identified" box
  3. 3 If it's urgent, write "MAINTENANCE REQUIRED" at the start
  4. 4 Include the location and what needs fixing
Safety First: If you find anything that could immediately harm children, block access to that area and contact your manager immediately.
[Screenshot would show: Issues reporting text box with example text like "MAINTENANCE REQUIRED: Loose bolt on swing seat - needs immediate attention"]

Finishing Your Inspection

  1. 1 Make sure all 8 checkboxes are completed
  2. 2 Review your notes and any issues reported
  3. 3 Click "Complete Inspection"
  4. 4 You'll see a confirmation message
[Screenshot would show: Complete inspection button and success message "Inspection completed for Holiday Park 1"]
What Happens Next: Your inspection is automatically saved and your manager will be notified of any issues that need attention.

Quick Reference Guide

Daily Inspection Checklist (8 Points)

  1. General Visual Inspection
  2. Surface Safety Check
  3. Equipment Integrity
  4. Perimeter Fencing Check
  5. Gate Operation Check
  6. Safety Signage Check
  7. Environmental Hazards
  8. Access Routes Clear

When to Report Issues

  • Any loose or damaged equipment
  • Broken fencing or gates that don't close
  • Missing or damaged safety signs
  • Trip hazards or surface problems
  • Any immediate danger to children

Emergency Contacts

For immediate safety concerns: Contact your site manager immediately and block access to the dangerous area.

Common Questions

What if I can't complete an inspection today?

Contact your manager as soon as possible. Daily inspections are required by law and cannot be skipped.

What if I find the same problem every day?

Continue reporting it each day until it's fixed. This creates a record that the issue is ongoing.

Can I go back and change an inspection?

No, once submitted, inspections cannot be changed. If you made an error, contact your manager.

What if the system won't let me log in?

Check your access code is correct. If problems continue, contact your manager for a new code.
