Your Holiday Park Awards safety entry. A short, positive set of questions that let your park be recognised for what it already does well.

Why these questions?

Every question here is modelled on the RoSPA Achievement framework, the most widely recognised model for occupational safety excellence in the UK. We use it as our assessment model so your recognition stands on a respected, consistent national standard rather than an arbitrary checklist. It is built to be positive: it asks you to show your leadership, your team's involvement, your proactive checks and your standout achievement of the year, not to catch you out. Responses are assessed independently and the score is indicative only, every entry carries a named human sign-off, and the awarding decision rests with the Holiday Park Awards panel.
