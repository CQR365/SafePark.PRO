Safe Working Procedures - Laundry Operations

Daily Equipment Startup
  1. Inspect washing machines for leaks or damage
  2. Check dryer lint traps and exhaust systems
  3. Test emergency stop buttons on all equipment
  4. Verify water temperature controls are functional
  5. Inspect electrical connections for wear or damage
Chemical Handling Protocol
  1. Wear appropriate PPE when handling detergents
  2. Store chemicals in designated secure areas
  3. Check COSHH data sheets before use
  4. Ensure adequate ventilation during operations
  5. Maintain spill kits in accessible locations
Manual Handling Best Practice
  1. Use mechanical aids for heavy laundry loads
  2. Maintain proper lifting posture at all times
  3. Take regular breaks to prevent strain injuries
  4. Team lift for loads exceeding individual capacity
  5. Report any back or joint discomfort immediately

  1. Adjust monitor to eye level and arm's length
  2. Position keyboard and mouse at elbow height
  3. Set chair height so feet rest flat on floor
  4. Ensure adequate back support and posture
  5. Check lighting to minimise screen glare
Daily Safety Checks
  1. Inspect workstation for hazards or damage
  2. Check electrical equipment for safety
  3. Ensure walkways are clear and unobstructed
  4. Verify emergency exits are accessible
  5. Report any maintenance issues promptly

  4. Stay hydrated and maintain comfortable temperature
  5. Report any discomfort or injury symptoms

Safe Working Procedures - Toilet Block Operations

Cleaning Protocol Standards
  1. Use colour-coded cleaning equipment for hygiene
  2. Clean from top to bottom, least to most contaminated
  3. Allow adequate contact time for disinfectants
  4. Rinse surfaces thoroughly after chemical application
  5. Document cleaning times and products used
Health & Safety Compliance
  1. Wear full PPE including gloves and aprons
  2. Wash hands thoroughly after each cleaning session
  3. Report any health concerns immediately
  4. Maintain first aid kit in accessible location
  5. Follow accident reporting procedures
Quality Assurance Checks
  1. Inspect all fixtures for proper operation
  2. Check toilet paper and hand soap supplies
  3. Verify adequate lighting and ventilation
  4. Test water temperature and pressure
  5. Complete quality checklist documentation
