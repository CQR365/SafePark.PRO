Open C1/C2
STL Mandatory Condition 2: Electrical installations must be inspected and tested at least every 5 years. A valid EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report) is required for licence application and renewal. EET (Electrical Equipment Testing) frequency is risk-based — annually for most commercial settings.

EICR Records
Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR): Must be carried out by a qualified electrician (Part P registered / NICEIC / NAPIT / SELECT in Scotland). Required every 5 years for STL properties. Result must be Satisfactory to operate. C1 observations require immediate remediation before guests are permitted.
EET Records
Electrical Equipment Testing (EET): Visual inspection and portable appliance testing of electrical equipment. Frequency is risk-based — annually for most STL properties. All equipment in each unit should be registered and tested. A pass label must be applied to each tested item.
Equipment Register
Equipment Register: Maintain a complete inventory of all electrical equipment per property. Each item should have a unique reference, be tested on the appropriate schedule, and have a current EET record. Failed or withdrawn items must be removed from service.
Remedial Actions
C1 observations require the installation to be made safe immediately — the property must not be used until the work is complete and verified. C2 observations require urgent remedial action. All actions must be documented with completion date and electrician sign-off.
