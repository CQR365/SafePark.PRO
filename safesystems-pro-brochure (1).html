Platform Features

Built for busy hospitality professionals who need reliable, efficient safety management tools.

Cloud-Based PWA
Access from any device, anywhere. Progressive Web App technology means no downloads required - works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
Real-Time Sync
Firebase-powered synchronisation ensures all team members see the latest data instantly across all locations.
Master Reporting
Generate comprehensive reports across all modules with one click. Filter by park, date range, and status for actionable insights.
Role-Based Access
Park-specific logins ensure staff only see relevant data. Admin codes provide multi-site oversight for management.
Smart Alerts
Dashboard badges highlight items needing attention. Never miss an overdue inspection, expiring certificate, or pending action.
Mobile Optimised
Designed mobile-first for staff on the ground. Complete inspections, log incidents, and update records from anywhere on-site.

Regulatory Compliance

Our modules are designed to help you meet key UK health and safety regulations.

Health & Safety at Work Act 1974
Core duty of care requirements for employers and premises operators.
RIDDOR 2013
Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations.
COSHH 2002
Control of Substances Hazardous to Health Regulations.
Fire Safety Order 2005
Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order requirements.
Food Safety Act 1990
Food hygiene, HACCP, and allergen management requirements.
Private Water Supplies
Scottish regulations for private water supply management.
LOLER 1998
