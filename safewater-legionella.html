HSG274 Requirement: Legionella risk assessments must be reviewed regularly and whenever there is reason to believe the current assessment is no longer valid — typically every 2 years, or following significant changes to the water system or building use.
Property Register
Duty Holder: The person responsible for the building and water systems (e.g. employer, person in control of premises). They must ensure the risk is assessed and controlled in accordance with L8 / HSG274.
Risk Assessments
Water Systems Inventory
HSG274 Part 2: Maintain an up-to-date written scheme for all water systems that present a risk of exposure to Legionella. Include cold water storage tanks, hot water calorifiers, all distribution pipework, showers, TMVs, and dead legs.
Temperature Monitoring Log
HSG274 Temperature Targets: Hot water — stored at ≥60°C, distributed at ≥50°C within 1 minute at outlets. Cold water — stored and distributed below 20°C. Blended outlets (TMVs) — typically 38-43°C at point of use.
Compliance Records
HSG274 Requirement: In addition to the risk assessment, duty holders must maintain records of: written schemes of control (per system), CWST inspections, TMV service records, disinfection records, and all corrective actions taken.
Written Schemes of Control
HSG274 Part 2, Para 2.70–2.76: A written scheme must be prepared for every water system that presents a risk. It must describe each element of the system, the safe operating parameters, frequency and methods of monitoring, and corrective action procedures. One scheme per system.
TMV Service Records
HSG274 Part 2, Para 2.29: Thermostatic mixing valves should be serviced annually. Service must include: thermal disinfection flush (60°C+ for minimum 1 minute), inline filter check and clean, set point verification (38–43°C), and re-commissioning. Individual records required per TMV.
Cold Water Storage Tank Inspections
HSG274 Part 2, Para 2.53: Cold water storage tanks must be inspected at least annually. The inspection must check: lid condition and screening, float valve operation, overflow and warning pipe condition, interior cleanliness, water temperature (target <20°C), insulation, and any signs of contamination or ingress.
Disinfection Records
HSG274: All thermal and chemical disinfection events must be documented, including pre- and post-disinfection sampling results where applicable, temperatures achieved, chemicals used, and clearance to return to service.
Legionella Sampling Log
HSG274 Action Levels (CFU/L for Legionella spp. in hot and cold water systems):
<100 CFU/L — Satisfactory: continue routine monitoring  |  100–999 CFU/L — Unsatisfactory: investigate, review control regime, resample within 2 weeks  |  ≥1,000 CFU/L — Immediate action: take system out of service, disinfect, do not return to service until <100 CFU/L confirmed
