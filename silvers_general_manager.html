1. The post

PurposeAccountable for the boatyard and marina operation, its plant, its people and the safety of customers and members of the public on site. (GSS-COM-ORG-001)
Reports toManaging Director
DepartmentSilvers Marine: the Company's boatyard and marina at Silverhills, Rosneath. Silvers Marine is a trading name and site of the Company, not a separate company.
Stand inTo confirm. If the post is confirmed, the Silvers Yard Manager would be the natural stand in.
DirectsTo confirm. If the post is confirmed: the Silvers Yard Manager and the Silvers Office Manager.
Status of the postTo confirm whether this is a post. The Silvers Yard Manager is now the senior Silvers post (vacant and being advertised, with a shareholder consultant standing in and signing off). The organisation document's entry under this title is carried by the Silvers Yard Manager profile as the working assumption.

Whether this post exists is to be confirmed, and the profile is written so that the Managing Director can decide. No law defines the title general manager of a boatyard or marina. The legal duties for the site fall on the Company as employer and as the person in control of the premises, and the Managing Director is the Managing Director. If the post is confirmed it would hold the business side of Silvers (customers, contracts, money, standards and resources) above the Yard Manager, who runs the yard. If it is not confirmed, everything below passes to the Silvers Yard Manager or stays with the Managing Director.

Authority of the post

The organisation document GSS-COM-ORG-001 Draft 3 gives four authority lines to the post it calls Silvers General Manager. On the working assumption they are carried by the Silvers Yard Manager and are shown in that profile, so nothing is listed here as held. Authority reserved to the Managing Director is not carried by this post or by anyone standing in for it.

  • None held on the working assumption. The four authority lines in the organisation document (stop any yard or marina operation, refuse or stop a lift, close an area of the site, refuse access on safety grounds) are shown in the Silvers Yard Manager profile.
  • If the post is confirmed: authority to be set by the Managing Director.
  • Limit: no authority to act as a director of the Company or to commit the Company beyond limits the Managing Director sets.
