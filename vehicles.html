8-Point Vehicle Inspection Checklist

0/8 completed
1. Lights & Indicators
All headlights, tail lights, brake lights, indicators, and hazard lights functioning correctly. Lens covers clean and undamaged.
2. Tyres / Tracks
Tyre pressure correct, tread depth adequate (minimum 1.6mm), no cuts, bulges or damage visible. Wheel nuts secure. For tracked vehicles: tracks properly tensioned and undamaged.
3. Brakes
Service brake and parking brake operating correctly. Brake pedal firm with no excessive travel. No unusual noises or vibrations when braking.
4. Fluid Levels
Engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering fluid, and windscreen washer fluid all at correct levels. No visible leaks under vehicle.
5. Mirrors / Visibility
All mirrors clean, undamaged and properly adjusted. Windscreen and windows clean with no cracks or damage affecting visibility. Wipers functioning correctly.
6. Horn / Warning Devices
Horn functioning correctly. Reversing alarm/beeper operational (if fitted). Warning lights and gauges functioning on dashboard.
7. Safety Equipment
Seatbelts present and functioning. First aid kit present and stocked. Fire extinguisher present and in date (if required). Warning triangle and hi-vis vest available.
8. General Condition
Bodywork free from significant damage. Doors, bonnet and tailgate secure and functioning. No unusual noises from engine or transmission. Vehicle clean and presentable.
