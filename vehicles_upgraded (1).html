Highland Holidays Vehicle & Plant Policy

1. General Policy

All vehicles and plant machinery operated within Highland Holidays Ltd and Overburns Farm must comply with this policy. This includes motor vehicles, agricultural machinery, construction plant, and all specialized equipment.

2. Authorization & Training

  • Only authorized personnel may operate vehicles and plant machinery
  • All operators must hold valid licenses and certifications for their assigned equipment
  • Regular training updates and competency assessments are mandatory
  • New equipment requires specific training before operation

3. Safety Requirements

  • Pre-use inspections must be completed for all equipment
  • Appropriate PPE must be worn at all times
  • Equipment must only be used for its intended purpose
  • All safety devices and guards must remain in place and functional

4. Maintenance & Inspection

  • Regular maintenance schedules must be followed
  • All statutory inspections (MOT, LOLER, etc.) must be current
  • Defects must be reported immediately
  • Equipment with defects must be taken out of service until repaired

5. Insurance & Documentation

  • All vehicles and plant must have current insurance cover
  • Documentation must be kept up to date in the SafePark.PRO system
  • Insurance claims must be reported within 24 hours
  • Regular reviews of insurance coverage are required

6. Environmental Compliance

All operations must comply with environmental regulations including emissions standards, fuel storage requirements, and waste disposal procedures.

7. Incident Reporting

All incidents, near misses, and accidents involving vehicles or plant must be reported immediately through the SafePark.PRO system.

Vehicle & Plant Incident Action Procedures

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

  1. Ensure Safety - Secure the scene and ensure no further danger
  2. Emergency Services - Call 999 if there are injuries or immediate danger
  3. First Aid - Provide first aid if qualified and safe to do so
  4. Isolate Equipment - Switch off and isolate any involved machinery

SECONDARY ACTIONS

  1. Notify Management - Contact site manager immediately
  2. Preserve Evidence - Do not move equipment unless essential for safety
  3. Document Scene - Take photographs if safe to do so
  4. Witness Statements - Identify and speak to any witnesses

REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

  • Report via SafePark.PRO incident system within 2 hours
  • Contact insurance provider within 24 hours
  • Notify HSE if reportable under RIDDOR
  • Complete detailed incident investigation report

CRITICAL REMINDER

Never attempt to restart or move damaged equipment. Always wait for qualified personnel to assess the situation.
